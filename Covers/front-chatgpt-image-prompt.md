# Front KISAH Universal Chapter Promo — ChatGPT Image Prompt

Use this prompt to generate the reusable FRONT promotional insert shown before every chapter of `My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts`.

Generate PNG first locally as `front.png`. After QA approval, manually convert the accepted PNG to `front.webp`. The PNG is a local/intermediate generation file only; store/use `front.webp` as the repository production asset.

```text
Create exactly ONE reusable FRONT KISAH promotional insert for the manga/manhwa series "My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts".

This same front insert will be reused before ALL chapters in the series.

It is NOT:
- a comic page
- a manga/manhwa panel
- a story cover
- a chapter title page
- a scene from Chapter 1
- a character illustration
- a browser/app screenshot

==================================================
1. BRAND LOCK — KISAH
==================================================

The platform/brand name is exactly:
KISAH

Never change the spelling.
Never write KISSAH, KISHA, KISAHH, or any other variation.

==================================================
2. FORMAT — HALF COMIC-PAGE HEIGHT
==================================================

Use exactly:
- width: 1024 px
- height: 768 px
- landscape 4:3 format

Do NOT:
- place a small banner inside a larger canvas
- use portrait or square orientation
- use 1024 x 1536
- add outer white margins
- add a fake phone/browser frame

If the production reader width changes later, preserve the principle that this promo shares the story-image width while remaining a compact half-height opening insert.

==================================================
3. PURPOSE
==================================================

This is the opening promotional card readers see before beginning a chapter.

Its job is to:
- introduce/reinforce the KISAH brand
- communicate that KISAH is a home for manga/manhwa love stories
- invite readers to discover and support more love stories
- feel integrated with this series without becoming a story scene

It must remain reusable before every chapter. Do not reference any chapter number, chapter title, chapter-specific event, cliffhanger, noodle scene, or temporary story state.

==================================================
4. EXACT PROMOTIONAL COPY — ONLY READABLE TEXT
==================================================

Use exactly this readable text and no other words:

KISAH
EVERY LOVE STORY DESERVES ANOTHER CHAPTER.
Discover and support manga love stories.

Do not paraphrase.
Do not add punctuation beyond what is written above.
Do not add a URL, QR code, username, hashtag, app-store badge, author name, chapter number, story title, copyright line, or extra CTA.

==================================================
5. TEXT HIERARCHY
==================================================

KISAH
- largest brand word
- instantly readable
- clean custom hand-drawn/editorial wordmark feeling
- matte solid color

EVERY LOVE STORY DESERVES ANOTHER CHAPTER.
- second-largest line
- emotionally strong headline
- fully readable at mobile width
- may wrap into two balanced lines without changing wording

Discover and support manga love stories.
- smaller supporting line
- still clearly readable
- calm and inviting

Do not use distressed horror lettering, glitch fonts, gothic fonts, chrome, 3D type, neon type, or cinematic title effects.

==================================================
6. STORY-SPECIFIC VISUAL IDENTITY — NO CHARACTERS
==================================================

Use only a restrained combination of story-wide motifs such as:
- flat dark-panel apartment/window geometry
- simple Seoul night-building silhouettes
- a small dead-device/power indicator icon
- clean external circuit-node lines
- a simple power-switch/breaker symbol
- optional plain brass-key silhouette
- restrained abstract black-pixel/static marks

Color direction:
- deep matte charcoal / blackout navy
- muted dark plum accent inspired by Nari
- restrained pale cyan-white circuit accent inspired by Hyun-woo
- optional tiny muted amber accent
- matte off-white/light neutral text

Do NOT show:
- Nari, Hyun-woo, Mrs. Na, Seungjae, or any person
- faces, bodies, hands, or human silhouettes
- story dialogue or spoilers
- hostile-system messages
- chapter-specific props/scenes
- noodles or food

==================================================
7. COMPOSITION
==================================================

Keep the promo clean and easy to read.

Recommended layout:
- text occupies roughly 55–70% of usable area
- story-specific decorative motif occupies roughly 30–45%
- generous negative space around copy
- KISAH reads first
- headline reads second
- supporting line reads third

Do not overcrowd the background, build an evidence wall, create an appliance advertisement, or create a fake KISAH website/app interface.

==================================================
8. ABSOLUTE VISUAL STYLE
==================================================

100% FLAT 2D HUMAN-DRAWN COLORED MANGA/MANHWA PROMOTIONAL ART.

Use:
- clean visible ink linework
- matte cel colors
- solid color blocking
- simple hard-edged shadows only where necessary
- flat geometric decorative motifs
- clean editorial typography

ABSOLUTELY NO:
- glossy webtoon shine
- cinematic rendering
- photorealism or semi-photorealism
- 3D/CG
- painterly/concept-art/airbrushed rendering
- film grain or gritty texture
- realistic material textures
- glass glare, chrome, or reflections
- gradients
- bloom, rim light, lens flare, volumetric light
- depth-of-field blur or atmospheric haze
- excessive glow

==================================================
9. SPOILER / REUSE LOCK
==================================================

Do not include:
- chapter number/title
- chapter-specific dialogue/event/cliffhanger
- Black Surge origin
- Hyun-woo origin
- culprit
- restoration mechanics
- future-story event
- time-sensitive release promise

==================================================
10. FINAL FRONT-PROMO ACCEPTANCE GATE
==================================================

Before accepting the image, verify:
1. exact outer size is 1024 x 768
2. brand spelling is exactly KISAH
3. exact readable copy is only the three approved lines
4. no other readable text/numbers/URL/QR/watermark/signature/logo appears
5. no people/characters/human silhouettes appear
6. design belongs to this blackout/electricity story through restrained motifs only
7. no chapter-specific scene or spoiler appears
8. artwork is strict flat matte 2D human-drawn manga/manhwa promotional art
9. text remains readable at mobile width
10. it reads as a reusable KISAH opening promotion, not a story panel/cover/product ad/fake app screenshot

ANY FAILURE = REJECT / CORRECT / REGENERATE.
```

## Usage Notes

- Generate/local-QA file: `front.png`.
- After approval, manually convert it to `front.webp`.
- Store and reuse only `front.webp` in GitHub/production after conversion; do not commit/re-attach the intermediate PNG as repository authority.
- Reuse the same approved `front.webp` before every chapter unless production intentionally changes the series-wide promotion later.
- Keep the brand spelling permanently locked as `KISAH`.
