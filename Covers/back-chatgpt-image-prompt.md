# Back KISAH Universal Chapter Promo — ChatGPT Image Prompt

Use this prompt to generate the reusable BACK promotional insert shown after every chapter of `My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts`.

Generate PNG first locally as `back.png`. After QA approval, manually convert the accepted PNG to `back.webp`. The PNG is a local/intermediate generation file only; store/use `back.webp` as the repository production asset.

```text
Create exactly ONE reusable BACK KISAH promotional insert for the manga/manhwa series "My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts".

This same back insert will be reused after ALL chapters in the series.

It is NOT:
- a comic page
- a manga/manhwa panel
- a story cover
- a chapter epilogue scene
- a character illustration
- a browser/app screenshot

==================================================
1. BRAND LOCK — KISAH
==================================================

The platform/brand name is exactly:
KISAH

Never change the spelling.
Never write KISSAH, KISHA, KISAHH, or any other variation.

==================================================
2. FORMAT — HALF COMIC-PAGE HEIGHT
==================================================

Use exactly:
- width: 1024 px
- height: 768 px
- landscape 4:3 format

Do NOT:
- place a small banner inside a larger canvas
- use portrait or square orientation
- use 1024 x 1536
- add outer white margins
- add a fake phone/browser frame

If the production reader width changes later, preserve the principle that this promo shares the story-image width while remaining a compact half-height closing insert.

==================================================
3. PURPOSE
==================================================

This is the closing promotional card readers see immediately after finishing a chapter.

Its job is to:
- keep emotional momentum after the chapter ends
- direct readers toward more KISAH love stories
- invite support for KISAH
- explain that support helps bring more chapters to readers more often
- feel connected to this series without becoming a story scene

It must remain reusable after every chapter. Do not reference any chapter number, chapter title, specific cliffhanger, or chapter-specific event.

==================================================
4. EXACT PROMOTIONAL COPY — ONLY READABLE TEXT
==================================================

Use exactly this readable text and no other words:

THE STORY DOESN'T END HERE.
READ MORE ON KISAH
More manga. More romance. More stories to fall for.
Support KISAH and help bring new chapters to readers more often.

Do not paraphrase.
Do not change `DOESN'T` or `KISAH`.
Do not add/remove words.

No URL, QR code, hashtag, username, app-store badge, author name, chapter number, story title, copyright line, or additional CTA.

==================================================
5. TEXT HIERARCHY
==================================================

THE STORY DOESN'T END HERE.
- emotional hook
- large and immediately readable
- matte solid lettering

READ MORE ON KISAH
- strongest CTA line
- visually emphasize KISAH without changing wording

More manga. More romance. More stories to fall for.
- medium supporting line
- rhythmic and inviting

Support KISAH and help bring new chapters to readers more often.
- smaller final support line
- sincere, not aggressive
- never rewrite into a guaranteed release-speed promise

Do not use distressed horror lettering, glitch fonts, gothic fonts, chrome, 3D type, neon type, or cinematic title effects.

==================================================
6. STORY-SPECIFIC VISUAL IDENTITY — NO CHARACTERS
==================================================

Use only a restrained combination of story-wide motifs such as:
- flat dark-panel apartment/window geometry
- simple Seoul night-building silhouettes
- a dead-device/power icon
- clean circuit-node lines
- a simple breaker/power-switch symbol
- optional plain brass-key silhouette
- restrained abstract black-pixel/static marks

Color direction:
- deep matte charcoal / blackout navy
- muted dark plum
- restrained pale cyan-white
- optional tiny muted amber accent
- matte off-white/light neutral text

Do NOT show:
- Nari, Hyun-woo, Mrs. Na, Seungjae, or any person
- faces, bodies, hands, or human silhouettes
- story dialogue
- hostile-system text
- specific cliffhanger imagery
- chapter-specific props/scenes
- noodles or food

==================================================
7. COMPOSITION
==================================================

Keep the insert clean, readable, and slightly more CTA-focused than the front promo.

Recommended layout:
- text occupies roughly 60–75% of usable area
- visual motif occupies roughly 25–40%
- generous safe margins
- strong central or left-aligned text block
- enough contrast for fast reading after a chapter

A story-specific circuit/power motif may form a subtle edge frame, corner decoration, or right-side visual anchor.

Do not overcrowd the background, use an evidence wall, make a TV/refrigerator product ad, or create a fake KISAH website/app interface.

==================================================
8. FRONT/BACK PAIR CONSISTENCY
==================================================

This BACK insert must look like the visual partner of the reusable FRONT KISAH insert.

Keep consistent between front and back:
- 1024 x 768 size
- flat matte manga/manhwa promotional rendering
- KISAH brand treatment
- blackout navy / dark plum / pale cyan-white palette family
- circuit-node decorative language
- text cleanliness
- overall production quality

The back can be slightly more energetic or CTA-forward, but must belong to the same promotional system.

==================================================
9. ABSOLUTE VISUAL STYLE
==================================================

100% FLAT 2D HUMAN-DRAWN COLORED MANGA/MANHWA PROMOTIONAL ART.

Use:
- clean visible ink linework
- matte cel colors
- solid color blocking
- simple hard-edged shadows only where necessary
- flat geometric decorative motifs
- clean editorial typography

ABSOLUTELY NO:
- glossy webtoon shine
- cinematic rendering
- photorealism or semi-photorealism
- 3D/CG
- painterly/concept-art/airbrushed rendering
- film grain or gritty texture
- realistic material textures
- glass glare, chrome, or reflections
- gradients
- bloom, rim light, lens flare, volumetric light
- depth-of-field blur or atmospheric haze
- excessive glow

==================================================
10. SPOILER / REUSE LOCK
==================================================

Do not include:
- chapter number/title
- chapter-specific cliffhanger/dialogue
- Black Surge origin
- Hyun-woo origin
- culprit
- restoration mechanics
- future-story event
- exact release schedule
- guaranteed release-speed promise

The line `Support KISAH and help bring new chapters to readers more often.` is the approved support wording.

==================================================
11. FINAL BACK-PROMO ACCEPTANCE GATE
==================================================

Before accepting the image, verify:
1. exact outer size is 1024 x 768
2. brand spelling is exactly KISAH every time
3. exact readable copy is only the four approved lines
4. no other readable text/numbers/URL/QR/watermark/signature/logo appears
5. no people/characters/human silhouettes appear
6. design belongs to this blackout/electricity story through restrained motifs only
7. no chapter-specific event, cliffhanger, or spoiler appears
8. artwork is strict flat matte 2D human-drawn manga/manhwa promotional art
9. all four text levels remain readable at mobile width
10. it clearly matches the front KISAH promo system
11. it reads as a reader CTA, not a story panel/cover/appliance ad/fake app screenshot

ANY FAILURE = REJECT / CORRECT / REGENERATE.
```

## Usage Notes

- Generate/local-QA file: `back.png`.
- After approval, manually convert it to `back.webp`.
- Store and reuse only `back.webp` in GitHub/production after conversion; do not commit/re-attach the intermediate PNG as repository authority.
- Reuse the same approved `back.webp` after every chapter unless production intentionally changes the series-wide promotion later.
- Keep the brand spelling permanently locked as `KISAH`.
