# Yoon Nari Canonical Flat-2D Reference Prompt

Use this prompt once to establish the production-safe canonical visual reference for Yoon Nari. This reference must be approved before final story-cover or interior-page generation begins.

```text
Create exactly ONE canonical character reference image for YOON NARI from "My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts".

PURPOSE
This image is a permanent production identity reference, not a story scene, cover, comic page, promotional banner, or storyboard. It exists only to lock Nari's face, hair, adult proportions, signature accessories, and flat-2D rendering for future generation.

LEGACY DESIGN RULE
The original/legacy story cover established Nari's intended character concept, but that old cover must NOT be attached or copied during normal production because its rendering is too cinematic, glossy, reflective, and over-rendered for the current visual standard. Preserve the established character concept below in a new clean flat-2D form. Do not imitate the legacy cover's lighting, gloss, composition, title, environment, or rendering.

IDENTITY LOCK — YOON NARI
- Korean woman, exactly 30 years old; unmistakably adult, never teenage or schoolgirl.
- Medium height, slim but sturdy practical build.
- Mature oval-to-soft-heart face with a compact jaw; not a childlike round face and not a glamorous model face.
- Sharp tired almond-shaped eyes with a slightly skeptical resting expression; faint under-eye shadows.
- Dark natural brows, clean and moderately straight.
- Small straight nose and natural medium lips; understated adult features.
- Tiny beauty mark beside the LEFT corner of her mouth; this is mandatory and must remain visible.
- Hair: SHORT ASYMMETRICAL DARK-PLUM SHAG. Layered, tousled, jaw/cheek-length silhouette with side-swept fringe and uneven longer pieces; one side subtly longer than the other. The nape remains short. Hair is dark plum, not black, brown, red, or purple neon.
- ABSOLUTELY NO long hair, shoulder-length hair, ponytail, long bob, or generic straight black heroine hair.
- Yellow scrunchie around one wrist.
- Noise-cancelling headphones around her neck.
- Signature modern game-designer clothing: oversized dark graphic hoodie under a cropped practical utility jacket, loose black trousers. Keep clothing matte, functional, contemporary, and slightly worn-in rather than fashionable/glamorous.
- Hold her sticker-covered tablet naturally against one side of her torso so the prop identity is visible without obscuring her face/body.
- Expression: exhausted, observant, skeptical, competent, mildly unimpressed. She is not smiling sweetly, posing seductively, or looking frightened.

POSE AND COMPOSITION
Create one single unified character-reference composition, not multiple comic panels. Show Nari from approximately knees-up in a relaxed neutral three-quarter standing pose, facing mostly toward camera so her face is large and readable. Keep both eyes, the beauty mark, hair silhouette, scrunchie, headphones, jacket/hoodie layering, and tablet visible.

BACKGROUND
Use a simple flat light neutral gray reference background with no apartment, no city, no furniture, no dramatic lighting, no story objects, and no typography except a tiny unobtrusive label `YOON NARI` only if absolutely necessary. Prefer no text.

ABSOLUTE STYLE LOCK
100% flat 2D human-drawn colored manga/manhwa character art.
- clean visible ink linework
- matte cel colors
- solid color blocking
- one controlled hard-edged cel-shadow tone
- natural flat skin color
- clean adult facial anatomy
- no rendering that imitates photography or 3D

ABSOLUTELY NO
- glossy webtoon shine
- shiny skin or lips
- glossy hair
- reflective clothing
- cinematic lighting
- rim light
- bloom
- lens flare
- volumetric light
- heavy gradients
- airbrushed shading
- photorealism
- semi-photorealism
- 3D/CG
- painterly rendering
- concept-art rendering
- depth-of-field blur
- reflective surfaces
- excessive texture
- neon accents

HARD REJECTION
Reject any Nari who has long black hair, a different generic heroine face, teenage proportions, missing beauty mark, missing yellow scrunchie, missing headphones, glamorous idol styling, school uniform styling, or glossy/cinematic rendering.

FINAL GOAL
Produce a clean, production-safe flat-2D canonical Nari whose identity can be attached to every later cover/page generation without importing the legacy cover's incorrect rendering style.
```
