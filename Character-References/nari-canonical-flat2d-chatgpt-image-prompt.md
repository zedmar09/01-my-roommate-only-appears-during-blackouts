# Yoon Nari Canonical Flat-2D Reference Prompt

```text
Create exactly ONE canonical character reference image for YOON NARI from "My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts".

PURPOSE
This image is a permanent production identity reference, not a story scene, cover, comic page, promotional banner, or storyboard. It exists only to lock Nari's face, hair, adult proportions, signature accessories, and flat-2D rendering for future generation.

LEGACY DESIGN RULE
The original/legacy story cover established Nari's intended character concept, but that old cover must NOT be attached or copied during normal production because its rendering is too cinematic, glossy, reflective, and over-rendered for the current visual standard. Preserve the established character concept below in a new clean flat-2D form. Do not imitate the legacy cover's lighting, gloss, composition, title, environment, or rendering.

IDENTITY LOCK — YOON NARI
- Korean woman, exactly 30 years old; unmistakably adult, never teenage or schoolgirl.
- Medium height, slim but sturdy practical build.
- Mature oval-to-soft-heart face with a compact, slightly angular lower face and compact jaw. Do not soften her into a generic round-faced heroine and do not give her glamorous model proportions.

- Hair: SHORT ASYMMETRICAL DARK-PLUM SHAG. Layered, tousled, irregular, jaw/cheek-length silhouette with side-swept fringe, sharper uneven side strands, and deliberately imperfect/messy layering. One side is subtly longer than the other. The nape remains short. The silhouette should feel slightly restless and asymmetrical rather than smooth, rounded, polished, or salon-perfect. Hair is dark plum, not black, brown, red, or purple neon.
- ABSOLUTELY NO long hair, shoulder-length hair, ponytail, long bob, smooth symmetrical bob, or generic straight black heroine hair.
- Yellow scrunchie around one wrist.
- Noise-cancelling headphones around her neck.
- Signature modern game-designer clothing: oversized dark graphic hoodie under a cropped practical utility jacket, loose black trousers. Keep clothing matte, functional, contemporary, and slightly worn-in rather than fashionable/glamorous.
- Hold her sticker-covered tablet naturally against one side of her torso so the prop identity is visible without obscuring her face/body.
- Expression: exhausted, observant, skeptical, competent, mildly unimpressed. She is not smiling sweetly, posing seductively, looking frightened, or presenting a soft generic romance-heroine expression.

The face must read as the same intended Nari identity, not merely a woman matching the wardrobe description. Prioritize:
- narrower tired almond eyes
- slightly sharper skeptical gaze
- compact/slightly angular lower face
- understated nose and lips

Do not beautify or soften these features into a generic attractive anime/manhwa heroine.

POSE AND COMPOSITION
Create one single unified character-reference composition, not multiple comic panels. Show Nari from approximately knees-up in a relaxed neutral three-quarter standing pose, facing mostly toward camera so her face is large and readable. Keep both eyes, the single beauty mark, hair silhouette, scrunchie, headphones, jacket/hoodie layering, and tablet visible.

BACKGROUND

No vignette, no gradient, no radial light, no spotlight, no texture, no shadow halo, no apartment, no city, no furniture, no dramatic lighting, no story objects, and no typography except a tiny unobtrusive label `YOON NARI` only if absolutely necessary. Prefer no text.
