# Na Young-sook Canonical Flat-2D Character Reference Prompt

Generate this reference once before any final Chapter 1 page that visually shows Mrs. Na. After QA approval, the approved flat-2D reference becomes the permanent visual identity source for Mrs. Na.

```text
Create ONE canonical character-reference sheet for NA YOUNG-SOOK / MRS. NA from "My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts".

PURPOSE
This is not a story page, cover, banner, or scene. It is a production character-identity reference that future comic pages will use to keep Mrs. Na visually identical.

STYLE HARD LOCK
100% flat 2D human-drawn colored manga/manhwa. Clean visible ink, matte cel colors, solid color blocking, and at most one controlled hard-edged cel-shadow tone. Neutral reference-sheet exposure.

ABSOLUTELY NO glossy rendering, cinematic lighting, bloom, rim light, lens flare, volumetric light, photorealism, semi-photorealism, 3D/CG, painterly rendering, airbrushing, reflective skin/hair/clothing, glass glare, strong gradients, depth-of-field blur, or over-rendering.

IDENTITY LOCK
Mrs. Na is a Korean woman in her late 60s. She must read as a practical, financially sharp older landlord who knows more than she admits, not as a witch, aristocrat, or comic caricature.

Preserve these canonical identifiers:
- late-60s Korean woman
- silver hair arranged in a tight practical twist
- mature facial structure with natural age lines, never youthful or glamorous
- observant, guarded eyes
- square jade earrings
- tailored mauve coat
- neat gloves
- old analog wristwatch
- physical metal key ring
- upright, brisk, self-controlled body language
- realistic older-adult proportions

REFERENCE LAYOUT
Use one clean portrait sheet with a plain neutral flat background. Show:
1. front/three-quarter bust view as the main image
2. small side-profile head view
3. small full-body standing view showing coat proportions, gloves, watch, earrings, and key ring

No story environment, dialogue, SFX, panel borders, dramatic pose, supernatural effect, text labels inside the artwork, or other characters.

FACIAL CONTINUITY
Keep a distinctive stable face suitable for reuse across pages. Avoid generic elderly-anime-face simplification. Do not make her cute, magical, villainous, regal, frail, or exaggerated.

COLOR LOCK
- silver hair
- natural Korean skin tone
- muted mauve coat
- jade-green earrings
- dark practical gloves/shoes
- restrained neutral palette

APPROVAL STANDARD
This image becomes canonical only after QA approval. Once approved, future pages must match the same face, hair twist, earrings, age, proportions, coat identity, and overall silhouette.

AVOID
Avoid witch styling, hanbok/palace clothing, fantasy jewelry, glowing eyes, sinister horror lighting, glamour makeup, youthful face, chibi, glossy hair, reflective jewelry, cinematic shadows, photorealism, 3D, painterly rendering, extra characters, props other than her canonical watch/key ring, and any text in the image.
```
