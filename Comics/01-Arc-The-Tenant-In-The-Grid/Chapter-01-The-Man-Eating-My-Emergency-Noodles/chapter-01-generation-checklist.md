# Chapter 01 Generation Checklist

Use this checklist when generating final Chapter 1 reader images from the ChatGPT image prompts.

## Before Generation

- Read `chapter-01-continuity-audit.md` before starting.
- Keep `../../../characters.md`, `../../style-guide.md`, and `../../prompt-template.md` open as reference locks.
- Confirm the reusable `../../../Covers/front-chatgpt-image-prompt.md` and `../../../Covers/back-chatgpt-image-prompt.md` prompts are present.
- Confirm the prompt set is complete: 14 page prompts in this chapter folder.
- Confirm the current chapter folder has no final reader PNGs/WebPs yet unless you are intentionally replacing approved images.
- Do not add future-arc explanations, culprit answers, or endgame logic in any generation instruction.
- Keep all outputs colored flat 2D human-drawn modern manga/manhwa: matte cel colors, clean linework, simple controlled shading, no glossy effect, no cinematic effect, no photorealism, no 3D render, and no painterly concept-art rendering.
- Keep the locked Unit 2407 layout, palette, refrigerator, television, smart-speaker, and fixed matte two-burner manual gas-cooktop designs unchanged across all pages; changing screen content must not redesign or move an appliance.
- Use one medium-bright neutral exposure for powered scenes and readable deep charcoal/muted blue-gray blocks for blackout scenes. Reject gradients, rim light, bloom, color grading, reflection streaks, glossy materials, crushed blacks, and unexplained brightness changes.
- Confirm every prompt includes `OBJECT SCALE AND SPATIAL LOGIC` and `TIME AND SCENE CONTINUITY`.
- Chapter 1 has no flashback. Reject any generated memory panel, dream border, past-event insert, or muted flashback treatment that is not in the script.

## Per-Image Approval Checks

Approve an image only if all applicable checks pass:

- It is colored flat 2D human-drawn modern manga/manhwa, not black-and-white, glossy, cinematic, photorealistic, 3D, or painterly.
- Front and back support banners are exact 1024 x 768 PNG banners in 4:3 landscape orientation, exactly half the height of a 1024 x 1536 story page at the same width.
- Reject any outer image size other than 1024 x 768, including portrait, vertical, square, long-scroll, full-page, full-height, or a small banner placed inside a larger canvas.
- Front and back banners have no panel-order numbers, no story-title cover layout, no plot synopsis, no QR codes, no browser/app UI, no URLs, and no extra fake logos.
- Front banner uses only the locked Unit 2407 matte cool-gray smart refrigerator and readable `SUPPORT KISAH`, with optional smaller subtitle `FOR MORE EXCITING LOVE STORIES.`
- Back banner uses only the locked Unit 2407 matte black television and readable `SUPPORT KISAH`, with optional smaller subtitle `FOR MORE EXCITING LOVE STORIES.`
- Reject people, characters, faces, hands, bodies, human silhouettes, character reflections, multiple hero appliances, appliance redesigns, hearts, flowers, rose petals, candles, wedding imagery, and hostile glitch styling.
- Panel-order numbers are present on interior pages and do not cover faces, speech bubbles, hands, props, or key clues.
- SFX are present only where specified by the prompt, match the situation, blend into the panel art, and do not cover faces, speech bubbles, hands, props, or key clues.
- Nari keeps dark-plum asymmetrical hair, yellow scrunchie, beauty mark, headphones, stickered tablet, adult proportions, and tired sharp expressions when visible.
- Hyun-woo keeps a matte black lower-face technical mask, low-tied long black hair, broad shoulders, dark sleeveless clothing, and matte black circuit-sigil tattoos with faint pale blue-white accents when physically visible.
- Hyun-woo is physically visible only during blackout pages. On Pages 13-14, he must be voice-only through speaker/static/device presence.
- Mrs. Na has silver hair in a tight twist, jade earrings, mauve coat, gloves, analog watch, and physical key ring.
- Seungjae has neat brown hair, fashionable office-casual clothing, smartwatch, wireless earbuds, and foldable phone.
- Unit 2407 remains a matte modern Seoul smart apartment, not a school, palace, gothic room, station, office tower boardroom, or fantasy set.
- Candlelight is warm matte amber, device text is limited cool cyan-white, dead-screen text is readable white, and Hyun-woo's tattoo accents are faint pale blue-white.
- Device text, speech bubbles, captions, notebook text, and SFX use only the specified words from the prompt and are visually blended into the correct material or sound source.
- Audible lines from the screenless smart speaker use readable speech bubbles with tails pointing to the speaker. Reject words printed on the speaker body or squeezed inside its indicator light.
- Hyun-woo's device messages use clean stable cyan-white lettering with minimal static and no crawling black pixels.
- Hostile system messages use stark white monospaced status lettering with broken glyph edges and crawling black pixels. Reject any image that makes the two signatures look identical.
- Recurring props keep their position/state unless a page explicitly moves them: brass key, electrical manual, A5 grocery notebook, stockpot speaker, lower-pantry emergency tray, desk tray, two knives, flashlight, radio, candle, matchbox, gas cooktop, noodle pot, chopsticks, pushed-back chair, refrigerator, and unplugged TV.
- Objects keep realistic scale relative to adult hands and furniture. Reject oversized manuals, keys, phones, knives, candles, noodle pots, speakers, notebooks, or characters. A close-up must change only the camera crop.
- Wardrobe follows the timeline: move-in outerwear on Pages 1-2, at-home clothes on Pages 3-5, after-work outerwear on Page 6, then the same charcoal hoodie, loose black trousers, and bare feet on Pages 7-14.
- A newly arriving character must be shown entering or waiting at the threshold before appearing inside the room.
- Every meaningful time jump must appear in its scripted dedicated full-width quiet matte gutter strip, separate from the numbered story panels. The strip contains only centered uppercase time text and no panel number, character, object, scenery, dialogue, SFX, inset, gradient, or decoration. Direct-continuation pages must have `NO TIME CARD` and no invented transition.
- Generated text is readable enough for the reader. Regenerate if important text is missing, misspelled, or replaced by fake extra writing.
- No author names, art credits, signatures, watermarks, app UI, unrelated logos, or large page numbers appear.
