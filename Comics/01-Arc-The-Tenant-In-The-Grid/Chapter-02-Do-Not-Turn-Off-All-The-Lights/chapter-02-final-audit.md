## Flat 2D / Rendering Rejection Gate — ABSOLUTE

Reject any generated Chapter 2 page showing:

- glossy webtoon shine or shiny plastic surfaces
- cinematic/film-style lighting or color grading
- photoreal or semi-photoreal faces/environments
- 3D/CG rendering
- painterly/airbrushed rendering
- mirror-like reflections on TV, refrigerator, windows, floors, or counters
- glass glare streaks
- bloom, rim light, halos, lens flare, volumetric rays
- soft gradient lighting/shading
- wet-looking floors/counters
- excessive electrical glow that lights the room
- depth-of-field blur
- over-rendered reflective hair/skin/appliances

Require clean human-drawn ink, matte cel colors, solid blocks, adult proportions, readable backgrounds, nonreflective appliances, and restrained hard-edged cel shading.
