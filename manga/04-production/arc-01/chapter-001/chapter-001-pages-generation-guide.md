# Chapter 001 Pages Generation Guide

## Status

**APPROVED PRODUCTION GUIDE — READY TO COMPILE PAGE PRODUCTION FILES.**

This file controls the generation workflow for all 36 pages of Chapter 001. It does not replace the story files under `manga/03-story/`; it translates their approved story rhythm into a repeatable page-generation process.

## Chapter Function

Chapter 001 begins Arc 01 as an ordinary apartment-security mystery.

Nari is provably away at her publishing-company workplace while the resident directly below her apartment hears footsteps and a dining-chair scrape from Nari's supposedly empty unit. The next morning the neighbor complains. Nari checks ordinary explanations and access history, finds no normal entry, later notices one dining chair slightly out of place, deliberately restores it to a known position, then continues an ordinary evening. After Nari leaves the living/dining area and is unaware of the room, the reader alone sees/hears that the chair moved again.

Chapter question:

**Who — or what — is using Nari's apartment when nobody is supposed to be inside?**

## Absolute Reveal Ceiling

Chapter 001 must never show or identify:

- Joo Hyun-woo
- a male silhouette
- a human hand/foot/body fragment belonging to the unseen presence
- a male voice from the apartment
- Hyun-woo's name
- a roommate claim
- the blackout manifestation rule
- a reader-facing explanation connecting electricity to the chair movement
- record/memory mythology
- wider city mechanism
- antagonist/origin/restoration material

The ending may show only the changed chair / restrained chair-scrape evidence.

## Supporting-Cast Decision For Chapter 001

**No reusable Hyejin visual reference is required for Chapter 001.**

Chapter 001 may use unnamed/background publishing-office coworkers for ordinary social proof. Any optional phone/friend voice during the domestic-night sequence remains offscreen and must not require a visible canonical character.

The downstairs neighbor is chapter-local. Establish one ordinary adult appearance on Page 006 and preserve it through Pages 007–010 using adjacent-page continuity. Do not create a full neighbor canonical for this chapter.

## Page Canvas Lock

All Chapter 001 first-generation pages use:

- orientation: portrait
- PNG review candidate: **1024 × 1536 px**
- aspect ratio: **2:3**
- reading direction: **right-to-left manga reading**
- black-and-white manga only
- consistent external page canvas across all 36 pages
- panel count/geometry changes according to story rhythm
- no vertical-webtoon dead space

After a PNG passes visual review, the user manually converts that exact accepted PNG to the matching `.webp` final authority.

Example:

`page-001.png` → approve → user converts → `page-001.webp`

## Absolute Human-Drawn Rendering Gate

Every Chapter 001 page must visibly read as a **real human-drawn black-and-white manga page**.

Default value/build order:

1. white paper / negative space
2. organic pencil/ink linework
3. light screentone
4. hand hatching/cross-hatching
5. sparse spot blacks only where genuinely needed

Required:

- natural line-weight variation
- slight human line imperfection allowed
- subtle pencil/sketch residue allowed if intentional and readable
- line-driven forms rather than shadow-driven digital modeling
- ordinary scenes remain light, readable and paper-white
- hatching must look hand-drawn rather than like a digital gradient mask

Reject:

- cinematic lighting
- film-still/storyboard look
- poster/key-art polish
- glossy webtoon/manhwa rendering
- plastic-clean/vector-like AI linework
- large default solid-black masses
- black cinematic vignettes
- ordinary scenes rendered as noir/high-contrast drama
- rim light, bloom, volumetric light, lens flare, depth-of-field blur, bokeh
- photorealism, painterly grayscale, CGI/3D-derived rendering

If a result looks more dramatic/polished/cinematic but less visibly human-drawn, **reject it**.

See:

- `manga/01-style/manga-style-lock.md`
- `manga/01-style/screentone-and-hatching-guide.md`
- `manga/04-production/page-production-standard.md`

## User-Supplied Page Sketch / Thumbnail Rule

If the user supplies or has already approved a page-specific sketch/thumbnail/layout image, attach it for that generation.

For the current page, the supplied sketch becomes the **highest composition authority** and controls:

- panel geometry/proportions
- panel order
- framing/camera intent
- character blocking
- major object placement
- negative space
- balloon-placement intent
- rough manga-page rhythm

Do not redesign, beautify, cinematicize, or “improve” an approved sketch.

The sketch controls composition only. Nari canonical still controls Nari identity, environment atlases still control reusable geometry, and story/page-production files still control narrative facts and script.

## Attachment Principle

Do not attach every available WebP to every page.

Use only the references that control something visible on the page:

- Nari visible → Style A + Style B + Nari canonical
- workplace visible → add workplace atlas
- apartment/corridor/elevator visible → add apartment atlas
- empty apartment → Style A + Style B + apartment atlas; do not attach Nari unless she is actually visible
- same scene continues → attach immediately previous approved page WebP when it materially helps pose, neighbor identity, environment angle, chair state, or visual seam continuity
- user-approved page sketch exists → attach it and follow its composition closely

More attachments are not automatically better. Irrelevant references can cause identity/environment drift.

## Style Priority

Use the approved paired references as complementary visual authorities:

- Style A: human-drawn manga line quality, anatomy, hands, facial construction, hatching discipline and controlled graphic accents
- Style B: adult-female rendering, ordinary life, domestic interiors, restrained expression, environment density/simplification and quiet suspense language

Nari's canonical overrides any generic human identity shown in Style A/B.

Environment atlases override any generic room/office shown in Style A/B.

A supplied page sketch/thumbnail overrides Style A/B for current-page layout/composition.

### Anti-drift interpretation

Style A/B must not be interpreted as permission to:

- increase solid-black coverage
- make ordinary pages darker
- add cinematic value structure
- add film lighting
- turn the page into poster/key art
- replace hand-drawn linework with polished digital rendering

For ordinary Chapter 001 pages, **white + line art must dominate**.

## Chapter-Wide Visual Rhythm

Chapter 001 is not a horror page sequence from beginning to end.

Required tonal progression:

1. ordinary adult work life
2. mundane neighbor irritation
3. rational security contradiction
4. practical inspection
5. weak chair clue
6. deliberate known-state setup
7. comfortable domestic decompression
8. quiet empty-room suspense
9. restrained reader-only physical proof

Use darker tone/hatching only when the scene earns them. Even suspense pages must remain recognizably hand-drawn manga rather than cinematic grayscale illustration.

## Sound / SFX Language

Keep Chapter 001 SFX restrained and physical.

Primary sounds:

- `THUMP` — human-weight floor movement / footstep transmitted downward
- `SKRRR` — dining chair/furniture scrape; the signature sound of Chapter 001
- `CLICK` — lock/light/small ordinary mechanism when needed
- `TAP` — phone/access-history interaction when useful
- `BEEP` — ordinary smart-lock/device acknowledgement when useful
- `BZZT` — optional phone vibration
- `SHHK` — optional curtain/fabric movement

Do not use giant supernatural impact lettering, electrical crackle, horror roar, or explanatory blackout SFX.

## Chair B Continuity Lock

The second dining chair is the physical continuity anchor.

Before Page 024:

- it may appear slightly displaced when Nari notices it
- Nari cannot prove who moved it

Pages 024–025:

- Nari deliberately pushes Chair B into the exact known/reference position encoded by the apartment atlas
- chair fully tucked at designated side
- chair back parallel to table edge
- table/floor geometry clearly proves alignment

Pages 025–034:

- Chair B must remain in that exact known position whenever visible
- no environment-generation drift may make it appear moved accidentally

Page 035:

- `SKRRR` occurs with source completely unseen

Page 036:

- Chair B is visibly displaced from the known position
- Nari does not witness this

## Automatic Chapter-Wide Rejection Conditions

Reject/regenerate any page that introduces:

- wrong Nari face/hair/body identity
- Nari copied from a generic Style A/B subject rather than her canonical WebP
- apartment geometry inconsistent with its master atlas
- workplace geometry/identity inconsistent with its master atlas
- wrong dining-chair count
- Chair B on the wrong designated side
- accidental Chair B movement before the scripted final movement
- visible Hyun-woo or any unexplained human body fragment from the hidden presence
- ghost/shadow-person imagery
- explicit blackout explanation
- invented supernatural effects
- premature horror intensity
- color, glossy webtoon finish, painterly grayscale, photorealism, CGI/3D
- cinematic lighting/value/composition drift
- film-still/storyboard/poster/key-art appearance
- vector-clean/plastic AI linework
- excessive solid-black masses or black vignette treatment
- ordinary scenes rendered as noir/high-contrast drama
- a supplied/approved page sketch materially ignored or redesigned
- page does not visibly read as human-drawn manga
- unreadable right-to-left panel order
- invented dialogue, labels, logos, or fake text not required by the page script
