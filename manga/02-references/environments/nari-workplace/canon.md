## Workplace Identity

- mid-sized publishing company in Seoul
- ordinary professional editorial office
- neither tiny indie room nor giant luxury corporate headquarters
- grounded contemporary environment
- no game-studio, software-lab, cyberpunk, startup-campus, broadcast newsroom, or detective-office styling

## Nari's Function In This Space

Nari is an editor. Her desk/work environment should naturally support manuscript reading, printed/digital proofs, correction notes, reference books, page review, coordination with coworkers/authors, ordinary calls/messages and deadline-driven late work.

The environment should communicate publishing/editorial work without readable brand names, manuscript titles, author names or logos.

## Recurring Zones

### Editorial Open Work Area

- Nari's recurring desk
- nearby coworker desks
- ordinary computers/monitors as tools, not the visual identity
- manuscripts/proofs/notebooks/office supplies
- modest books/reference shelves
- printer/copier or ordinary publishing-office equipment only where useful

Nari's desk location and orientation must remain stable across views.

### Small Editorial Review / Meeting Area

- modest table/chairs or small enclosed/semi-enclosed meeting room
- supports manuscript/proof discussions and ordinary coworker scenes
- not a high-tech presentation room

### Late-Night Office State

Same geometry as daytime.

- fewer coworkers
- quieter ordinary office state
- believable deadline work after most staff leave
- no cinematic horror transformation
- Chapter 001 uses this environment to prove Nari is away from home at the time of the apartment noise

## Visual Atmosphere

- practical
- adult
- mildly busy during day
- papers/books/proofs provide texture without chaotic clutter
- believable deadline culture
- ordinary contemporary furniture

## Chapter 001 Continuity

The cold open must establish Nari at this office clearly enough that the reader accepts she was not home when the downstairs neighbor heard activity.

The environment atlas itself does not encode story timestamps or named story characters.
