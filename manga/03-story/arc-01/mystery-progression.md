# Arc 01 Mystery Progression

## Status

**DRAFT FOR USER REVIEW — NOT YET CANON-LOCKED.**

## Mystery Design Principle

Arc 01 answers only the **first layer** of the series mystery: whether Hyun-woo is a genuine recurring blackout-linked physical presence and whether Nari can coexist with him.

It does not explain the true cause.

## Information Layers

### Layer 0 — Dismissible irregularity

Examples:

- reset clock
- brief flicker
- moved chair
- relay/power irregularity

These must have ordinary explanations available.

### Layer 1 — Undeniable presence

A human figure is physically present in Nari's apartment during a genuine blackout.

### Layer 2 — Observable condition

The presence appears correlated with genuine loss of ordinary supplied power and ends when normal power returns.

### Layer 3 — Evidence inconsistency

Attempts to prove, record, or explain the presence do not behave like ordinary intruder evidence.

### Layer 4 — History contradiction

Hyun-woo knows or claims a connection to the residence/building that available records do not support.

### Layer 5+ — Reserved For Later Arcs

- wider city pattern
- memory/identity erasure mechanism
- buried incident
- true cause/opposition
- restoration rules/cost
- endgame choice

Arc 01 may only **glimpse** Layer 5 at its ending.

## Chapter 001 Mystery State

### What Actually Happens — Writer Knowledge

A genuine power-loss event creates the condition in which Hyun-woo can be physically present in Nari's apartment. He has prior familiarity with the residence and has become aware that Nari is the current tenant.

The exact reason, duration mechanics, origin, and limits remain intentionally undefined at Chapter 001 level.

### What Nari Believes Before The Blackout

- she lives alone
- the apartment has minor electrical quirks
- any small object-position oddity is probably her own fatigue or habit

### What Nari Believes At Chapter End

- an unknown man is inside her home
- he may have been there before
- he claims a prior connection to the apartment
- immediate assumption remains **intruder/security threat**, not supernatural roommate

### What The Reader Knows At Chapter End

- the electrical irregularity and stranger are probably connected because of genre/premise placement
- the stranger's claim conflicts with Nari's understanding of her home
- no rule or origin has been proven

### What Hyun-woo Knows / Withholds

- he knows the apartment and recognizes Nari as the current resident
- he does not volunteer his origin
- he does not explain the blackout condition
- he does not provide a full name/history in Chapter 001

### Fair Clues Planted

- a previously normal-looking electrical reset/flicker
- the same dining chair being slightly out of Nari's preferred position more than once
- Hyun-woo's final claim that Nari moved into **his** apartment

### Deliberately Withheld

- how he entered
- whether he can leave
- why blackout matters
- what happens when power returns
- whether anyone else can see him
- whether records support his claim

## Early Arc Progression

### Chapter 002

**Answer:** ordinary power return coincides with Hyun-woo's absence.

**New question:** did he vanish, escape, or exploit something Nari missed?

### Chapter 003

**Answer:** normal security logic does not produce a satisfying entry/exit explanation.

**New question:** what condition is actually reproducible?

### Chapters 004–005

**Answer:** genuine supplied-power loss is the relevant observable condition; ordinary lights-off behavior is not sufficient.

**New question:** why is Hyun-woo tied to that condition?

### Chapters 006–010

**Answer:** he can interact as a physical person during valid windows, but proof/evidence is unreliable.

**New question:** why does evidence fail?

### Chapters 011–013

**Answer:** Hyun-woo has real knowledge of the residence/building that predates Nari's tenancy.

**New question:** what was his relationship to this place?

### Chapters 014–017

**Answer:** at least one anomaly is not fully explained by Nari's apartment alone.

**New question:** how large is the phenomenon?

### Chapters 018–020

**Answer:** Nari and Hyun-woo can trust each other enough for provisional coexistence.

**Arc-ending contradiction:** a credible identity/history check does not support something Hyun-woo should be able to prove.

**Next-layer question:** why can a physically real person be absent from the ordinary record of the world?

## Anti-Shortcut Rule

Never resolve a newly opened layer in the same scene merely to provide a bigger climax. Let consequences, tests, wrong theories, ordinary-life pressure, and relationship choices occupy the space between question and answer.
