# Arc 01 Mystery Progression

## Status

**APPROVED OPENING DIRECTION — ACTIVE REVEAL GUARDRAIL.**

## Mystery Design Principle

Arc 01 must not begin by proving the title premise.

The reader and Nari should move through progressively harder explanations:

**noise → unauthorized activity → security contradiction → physical-state contradiction → electrical correlation → unseen human presence → voice → human reveal → observable condition → evidence inconsistency → history contradiction**

Each layer should have room for consequences, wrong theories, ordinary-life pressure, and character choices before the next layer is confirmed.

---

## Information Layers

### Layer 0 — Dismissible Household Oddity

Examples:

- object not exactly where remembered
- neighbor noise that might belong to another unit
- minor clock reset/flicker
- ordinary building noise

No supernatural conclusion is justified.

### Layer 1 — Unauthorized Activity Problem

Someone appears to be using Nari's apartment while she is away or unaware.

The evidence is behavioral/physical, not yet a visible person.

### Layer 2 — Security Contradiction

Door/access logic does not show a normal entry that explains the activity.

Mundane alternatives still exist.

### Layer 3 — Repeatable Physical-State Contradiction

Nari deliberately leaves an object/environment state and later finds it changed without a normal entry explanation.

This proves only that something is wrong with her current theory.

### Layer 4 — Electrical Correlation

Disturbances cluster around genuine supplied-power irregularities.

Correlation is observed before causation is claimed.

### Layer 5 — Unseen Human Presence

During a substantial real blackout, Nari directly experiences unmistakably human physical cues without seeing the source clearly.

### Layer 6 — Communication

The presence speaks/responds intelligently.

Voice precedes full visual identity.

### Layer 7 — Human Reveal

A stable adult human appearance is finally established.

Name/identity facts may begin here, but origin remains protected.

### Layer 8 — Observable Condition

Nari learns through testing that genuine supplied-power loss matters and simply making a room dark is not equivalent.

### Layer 9 — Evidence Inconsistency

Attempts to preserve normal proof of the presence behave incompletely or incorrectly.

### Layer 10 — History Contradiction

Hyun-woo knows or claims a connection to the residence/building that available records do not support cleanly.

### Layer 11+ — Reserved For Later Arcs

- wider city pattern
- identity/memory rejection mechanism
- buried accident/history
- true cause/opposition
- restoration mechanics/cost
- endgame choice

Arc 01 may open the door to Layer 11 near its ending but must not explain it.

## Chapter 001 Mystery State

### What Actually Happens — Creator Knowledge

- while Nari is away, activity occurs inside her apartment during a valid brief supplied-power-loss window
- a neighbor hears footsteps / furniture movement
- no ordinary entry is recorded
- later, after Nari deliberately establishes a known chair position and goes to sleep, another brief valid supplied-power-loss window permits the chair to move again
- the exact actor is not shown or named

This creator knowledge must not be translated into reader-facing explanation.

### What Nari Believes At Start

- she lives alone
- the apartment is ordinary
- neighbor complaints / building noise should have mundane causes

### What Nari Believes After Morning Complaint

- neighbor may have mistaken the source
- someone could have entered without her realizing
- the neighbor may have the time wrong

### What Nari Believes After Access Check

- ordinary entry data does not support the simplest intruder theory
- data could still be incomplete or wrong

### What Nari Believes At Chapter End

Nari does **not** know the chair moved after bedtime.

Her current question remains practical:

**Did someone enter my apartment, or is some ordinary explanation making the evidence look stranger than it is?**

### What The Reader Knows At Chapter End

- Nari was genuinely away during the original noise
- ordinary access history did not show a normal entry
- Nari deliberately established a known chair position
- the chair later moved when Nari was not watching

The reader knows Nari's fatigue/memory is not enough to explain everything, but does not know the actor or condition.

### What Hyun-woo Knows / Withholds — Creator Continuity Only

Hyun-woo is not reader-facing in Chapter 001.

Do not use his internal knowledge to leak explanation into narration, shadow imagery, or dialogue.

## Early Arc Progression

### Chapter 002

**Answer:** the chair/object discrepancy is real enough for Nari to take seriously.

**New question:** how can physical activity happen without normal entry evidence?

### Chapter 003

**Answer:** at least one low-tech known-state test changes unexpectedly.

**New question:** is someone bypassing the apartment's ordinary security model?

### Chapter 004

**Answer:** strongest mundane theory explains only part of the evidence and fails on one specific fact.

**New question:** what variable is Nari missing?

### Chapter 005

**Answer:** disturbances loosely correlate with power resets/flickers/interruptions.

**New question:** coincidence or condition?

### Chapter 006

**Answer:** a physical change overlaps a genuine electrical interruption strongly enough to make the correlation serious.

**New question:** what exists or becomes possible during these windows?

### Chapter 007

**Answer:** Nari directly experiences an unseen human presence during a substantial blackout.

**New question:** who is there?

### Chapter 008

**Answer:** the presence can communicate intelligently.

**New question:** is the speaker a normal intruder under impossible conditions, or something else?

### Chapters 009–010

**Answer:** a stable human identity/appearance begins to emerge.

**New question:** why can this person only be present under abnormal power conditions?

### Chapters 011–013

**Answer:** genuine supplied-power loss is the observable condition; normal proof of the person is unreliable.

**New question:** why does evidence fail and what is his connection to this place?

### Chapters 014–017

**Answer:** repeated interactions make practical coexistence possible; Hyun-woo knows details he should not know.

**New question:** what is his real history with the apartment/building?

### Chapters 018–020

**Answer:** Nari and Hyun-woo can trust each other enough for provisional coexistence.

**Arc-ending contradiction:** a credible identity/history check fails to support something a physically real person should be able to prove.

**Next-layer question:** why can the ordinary world fail to account for someone Nari has physically interacted with?

## Anti-Shortcut Rules

1. Do not reveal a person simply because moved-object suspense has lasted one chapter.
2. Do not explain the blackout condition in the same chapter where Nari first notices an electrical correlation.
3. Do not give Hyun-woo a full visual reveal in the same scene as his first voice unless later pacing review strongly justifies it.
4. Do not solve proof/evidence failure in the first chapter where it occurs.
5. Do not answer Hyun-woo's origin in Arc 01.
6. Every delayed answer must be filled with changing consequences, theories, tests, relationships, or parallel events — never empty repetition.
