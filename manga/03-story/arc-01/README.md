# Arc 01

**Status: OPENING DIRECTION APPROVED — ARC NAME AND LATER DETAILS STILL IN DEVELOPMENT.**

Arc 01 is rebuilt from the clean manga premise. No retired Manhwa arc title, chapter list, event order, or reveal timing is inherited automatically.

## Approved Arc Function

Arc 01 is the **threshold / presence / coexistence arc**.

It begins as a grounded apartment-security mystery and only gradually earns the blackout-roommate premise.

The early progression is intentionally slow-burn:

1. unexplained activity while Nari is away
2. security/access contradiction
3. repeated physical-state contradiction
4. electrical correlation
5. unseen human presence during a real blackout
6. first voice
7. partial then clear human reveal
8. observable condition testing
9. evidence/history problems
10. negotiated coexistence

## Reveal Protection

Arc 01 must not spend:

- Hyun-woo's full origin
- final antagonist/cause
- full memory/record mechanism
- citywide explanation
- restoration mechanics
- final romance/endgame stakes

The arc should end with **provisional coexistence plus one larger contradiction**, not the solution to the series mystery.
