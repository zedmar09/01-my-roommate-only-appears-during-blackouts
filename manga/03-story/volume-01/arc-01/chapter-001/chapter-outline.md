# Chapter 001 Outline

## Status

Not written yet.

## Design Requirements

The chapter must:

- function as a strong first manga chapter, not a lore tutorial
- introduce a compelling immediate human problem
- establish tone before explaining mechanics
- create at least one unanswered question worth continuing for
- use page turns intentionally
- allow parallel or off-screen events if they strengthen suspense
- avoid dumping the old apartment rules in exposition
- end with a material change, discovery, encounter, or decision

## Open Opening Options

These are possibilities, not canon:

- begin with an event before Nari reaches the central location
- begin after she has already been living there
- begin from another character's viewpoint
- begin with the aftermath of a blackout
- begin with two simultaneous incidents that appear unrelated
- begin with an ordinary work/social problem that collides with the supernatural later

Choose the strongest opening only after the new Arc 1 engine is defined.
