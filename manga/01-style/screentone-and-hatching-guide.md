# Screentone and Hatching Guide

## Purpose

Values are created through black ink, white paper, screentone, and hatching—not color gradients.

## Use

- light tones for subtle clothing/material separation
- mid tones for interior depth and ordinary shadow
- darker tones for night, suspense, and focus
- solid black for silhouette, framing, hair masses, deep shadow, horror, and graphic emphasis
- hatching for texture, tension, fatigue, darkness, rough surfaces, and hand-drawn atmosphere

## Restraint

Do not cover every surface with tone. Preserve white areas so the page breathes and focal points remain readable.

Avoid photographic grayscale shading, smooth digital airbrush gradients, or glossy rendered light.
