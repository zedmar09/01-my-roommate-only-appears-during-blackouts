# SFX Lettering Guide

## Global Language Rule

Follow:

`manga/01-style/reader-visible-language-lock.md`

All reader-visible SFX in the active manga are **English / Latin-letter SFX only** unless the user explicitly approves a page-specific exception.

Do not translate or convert SFX into Japanese kana/kanji, Korean hangul, Chinese characters, or invented pseudo-language glyphs.

Examples of approved Chapter 001 SFX language include:

- `THUMP`
- `SKRRR`
- `CLICK`
- `TAP`
- `BEEP`
- `BZZT`
- `SHHK`

The exact page-production MD controls which SFX, if any, may appear.

## Visual Integration

SFX should feel hand-integrated into the manga art.

Use scale, weight, angle, and placement to communicate intensity. Small ordinary sounds may sit near the source; major impacts may become part of the composition.

Do not overuse SFX. Silence is a valid storytelling choice.

## Layout References

Layout references should normally omit SFX lettering entirely. If SFX placement must be reserved, leave a clear empty region or non-readable placement marker rather than generating letters.

## Final Pages

Final pages may use only the exact approved English SFX written in the page-production MD.

Reject if:

- SFX is translated
- non-English lettering appears
- fake glyphs appear
- an unapproved SFX is invented
- approved English SFX wording changes

Temporary translation/romanization notes belong only in production documentation, never in reader-facing art.
