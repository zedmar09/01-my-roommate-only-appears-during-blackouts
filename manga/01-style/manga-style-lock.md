# Manga Style Lock

## Absolute Direction

Create a **black-and-white, hand-drawn manga page**, not a colored webtoon/manhwa strip.

Required visual language:

- confident pencil/ink line art with visible human-drawn character
- varied contour and interior line weight
- selective roughness and natural imperfection
- white paper as an active compositional value
- rich solid blacks when needed
- screentones for value, atmosphere, fabric, skin shadow, and environment depth
- hatching/cross-hatching where appropriate
- speed lines, impact lines, emotion marks, simplified reaction drawings, and visual exaggeration when the scene calls for them
- detailed establishing backgrounds when location matters
- reduced/simplified backgrounds when dialogue, comedy, or emotion is the focus
- natural adult anatomy and readable hands

Reject:

- color
- flat-color webtoon rendering
- glossy highlights
- cinematic color grading
- bloom, lens flare, volumetric lighting
- CGI / 3D / game-render appearance
- photorealism or semi-photorealism
- painterly concept art
- airbrushed skin or hair
- plastic-clean AI finish
- fixed vertical-scroll gutters or giant webtoon dead space
- identical panel grids repeated page after page

## Reference Vibe

The intended reference feeling is conventional published manga: irregular but controlled panel sizes, frequent close-ups and cut-ins, dialogue balloons integrated into composition, dense and sparse pages alternating, and the freedom to show several events or viewpoints in one chapter.

Do not copy any specific copyrighted character, panel, dialogue, logo, or composition from a reference manga. Match only the broad production language and medium.
