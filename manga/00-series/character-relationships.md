# Character Relationships

## Core Working Relationship

### Yoon Nari ↔ Joo Hyun-woo

Series center: two adults forced into an impossible form of cohabitation/connection because Hyun-woo cannot exist normally under ordinary power conditions.

Nari is a 30-year-old publishing-company editor with an ordinary adult life that must continue while the mystery grows. She does not begin the series as an investigator or technical specialist.

The new version must build Nari and Hyun-woo's relationship through specific choices, conflict, trust, inconvenience, shared goals, secrets, and limited time together. Do not assume the old first meeting or old romance progression.

Chapter 001 does not show or identify Hyun-woo. The relationship cannot begin on-page until the approved slow-burn reveal order earns his presence.

## Koo Hyejin — Rebuild Direction

Hyejin may be retained as Nari's trusted normal-world friend/coworker anchor because that relationship is useful for showing Nari outside the supernatural mystery.

Do **not** inherit the retired technical/QA occupation automatically. If retained visually, Hyejin's exact role within or around Nari's publishing-world life and her new manga design must be approved before generating her canonical reference.

## Neighbor Below Nari

The Chapter 001 complaint comes from the resident **directly below Nari's apartment**. This person is currently a functional minor character rather than a locked long-term supporting cast member.

Do not create a full reusable neighbor atlas unless later Arc 01 planning gives this person a recurring goal or story function.

## Supporting Relationship Rule

Add a supporting relationship to canon only after the character has a clear independent motivation and long-form function. Ordinary friends, coworkers, neighbors, editors, authors, building staff, and other people should not exist only to deliver supernatural exposition.
