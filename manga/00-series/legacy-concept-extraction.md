# Legacy Concept Extraction

This file is a firewall between the retired Manhwa continuity and the new manga canon.

## Recoverable Through Git History

All removed Manhwa assets, color references, strip prompts, legacy chapter plans, and old root canon files remain available in Git history. They are intentionally not copied into an `archive/` folder so the active repository stays clean.

## Premise-Level Ideas Worth Re-evaluating

These are **candidates**, not automatically binding details:

- adult protagonist living/working in present-day Seoul
- mysterious roommate whose physical existence depends on real power loss
- inability of ordinary records/cameras/systems to verify him
- romance constrained by limited physical time together
- electricity, infrastructure, devices, records, and memory as connected mystery motifs
- an apartment or other recurring home base as an emotional and supernatural anchor
- a broader city-scale reason for the roommate's impossible condition
- supporting characters who can investigate separate threads outside the protagonist's immediate experience

## Retired Production Assumptions

Do not inherit:

- full-color flat-2D manhwa styling
- vertical-scroll strip construction
- deterministic 32-strip Chapter 1
- old canonical WebP character appearances
- old environment/object image packs
- old Arc 1 chapter titles and fixed 8-arc/120-chapter roadmap
- legacy Chapter 1 scene order, dialogue, pacing, or reveals

## Re-approval Rule

A legacy idea becomes new canon only after it is deliberately written into an active file under `manga/00-series/` or an approved chapter under `manga/03-story/`.
