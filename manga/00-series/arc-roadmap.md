# Arc Roadmap

## Long-Form Direction

The previous fixed 8-arc / 120-chapter Manhwa roadmap is retired.

The new manga is designed to sustain **100+ chapters**, but the exact number of arcs and chapters is not fixed in advance. Story shape determines length.

## Arc 01 — TITLE PENDING

**Status:** OPENING DIRECTION APPROVED / FULL ARC STILL IN DEVELOPMENT  
**Soft length target:** approximately 18–22 chapters  
**Series function:** threshold / presence / coexistence arc

Arc 01 now begins as a grounded apartment-security mystery rather than immediately revealing the blackout-roommate premise.

Approved escalation order:

1. unexplained activity while Nari is absent
2. access/security contradiction
3. repeatable physical-state contradiction
4. electrical correlation
5. unseen human presence during a genuine blackout
6. first voice
7. partial then clear human reveal
8. observable condition testing
9. proof/history inconsistencies
10. negotiated provisional coexistence

Arc 01 must not resolve the complete origin, record/memory mechanism, citywide cause, final antagonist, restoration method, or endgame romance.

`manga/03-story/arc-01/`

## Future Arcs

Do not name or lock later arcs yet.

A future arc should exist because the previous arc's local answer creates a new dramatic problem. Do not invent arcs merely to reach a chapter quota.
