## Chapter 001 — Objective Event Order

1. Nari is living alone in a recently rented apartment and has established ordinary routines.
2. On the night before the main Chapter 001 day, Nari remains away from home late at work / another provable location.
3. During an overlapping time window, a neighbor hears footsteps / human-weight movement and a chair/furniture scrape from Nari's apartment.
4. Nari returns home later, unaware that a neighbor heard anything unusual.
5. The next morning, neighbor complains about the late-night noise and gives the approximate time.
6. Nari realizes she was not home at that time.
7. During the day, Nari verifies her own away-timeline and checks ordinary apartment access history.
8. No ordinary entry appears during the relevant absence window.
9. Nari considers mundane explanations and continues her normal day.
10. That evening, Nari returns home and performs a practical inspection.
11. Nothing obvious is stolen/damaged and no person is found.
12. Nari notices the dining chair is slightly out of her expected position but cannot prove she did not move it herself.
13. Nari deliberately places the chair in a precise known position.
14. Nari lives through an ordinary evening; the chair remains in that known position whenever visible.
15. Nari leaves the living/dining area and eventually becomes unaware of it.
16. During the final-night window, a brief genuine supplied-power interruption may occur off-panel as creator continuity.
17. During/around that valid hidden window, the dining chair changes position.
18. The reader sees/hears the final chair movement; Nari does not.
