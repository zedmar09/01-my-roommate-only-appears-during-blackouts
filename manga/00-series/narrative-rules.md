# Narrative Rules

1. The manga is not required to follow one character continuously.
2. Separate events may occur simultaneously and intercut across pages.
3. Supporting characters can carry scenes without the protagonist present.
4. Every recurring story thread needs its own goal, information state, and consequences.
5. Exposition should be earned through action, conflict, discovery, or conversation with a reason to happen.
6. Do not explain a mystery simply because the reader has seen it once.
7. Page turns are storytelling tools: place reveals, reversals, entrances, and visual shocks deliberately.
8. Panel count is story-dependent. Dense dialogue pages, sparse dramatic pages, reaction pages, action pages, and full-page reveals are all valid.
9. Chapters should change the situation, not merely add clues.
10. The previous Chapter 1 sequence is not a template.
11. The series is designed for 100+ chapters. Early chapters must not spend answers that belong to later mystery layers simply to create a fast climax.
12. Reveal progression should move through layers: **presence → observable condition → trust/coexistence → evidence/record anomalies → wider city pattern → buried history → true cause/opposition → final restoration/consequence**. The exact arc placement remains flexible.
13. A chapter may answer a local question while opening or sharpening another. Avoid answering several major mystery layers in a single installment.
14. Slow-burn does not mean stalled. Every chapter must advance at least one of: situation, character goal, relationship, investigation, consequence, or active parallel thread.
15. Romance develops through familiarity, conflict, choices, vulnerability, and accumulated domestic experience. Do not shortcut from first encounter to emotional intimacy.
16. Repetition must change context. A later blackout should create a different problem, choice, clue, relationship shift, or consequence rather than replaying the first encounter.
17. Preserve information asymmetry. The reader, Nari, Hyun-woo, supporting characters, and unseen opposition may know different things at the same time.
