# Tone and Genre

## Target Blend

- adult supernatural mystery
- urban fantasy
- romantic comedy / slow-burn intimacy
- suspense and occasional horror
- grounded everyday life

## Tone Principle

The supernatural should feel stranger because the surrounding life is ordinary. Humor should come from character behavior, timing, inconvenience, and relationship friction rather than breaking the reality of the mystery.

Allow tonal contrast: a funny conversation can sit beside an ominous cutaway; a quiet domestic page can follow a high-density investigation page; a frightening reveal can be delayed by mundane work or social obligations.

Avoid turning every scene into horror, every romance beat into melodrama, or every device into a haunted gag.
