# Continuity Ledger

Central record of facts that have become binding in the new manga canon.

## Current Locked Facts

- active format is black-and-white page-based manga
- retired Manhwa production is non-canon as story sequencing and visual authority
- working title remains `My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts`
- core premise links the mysterious roommate's physical presence to genuine electrical blackouts / supplied-power loss
- Chapter 1 is rebuilt from zero rather than adapting the retired Chapter 1
- the series is intentionally designed to sustain **100+ chapters** with smooth progression rather than an early central-mystery climax
- story progression uses layered reveals, multiple event threads when useful, and arc-level local resolutions instead of dumping origin/cause answers into early chapters
- Arc 01 title remains intentionally pending

## Chapter 001 — Approved Story Direction

The opening mystery is **activity inside Nari's supposedly empty apartment**, not the immediate reveal of the blackout roommate premise.

Locked Chapter 001 facts:

- Nari already lives in the apartment and has an established routine; Chapter 1 is not a move-in chapter
- the opening uses two simultaneous locations: Nari is provably away from home while a neighbor hears footsteps / furniture movement from Nari's apartment
- the next morning the neighbor complains about late-night noise
- Nari's strongest immediate contradiction is simple: **she was not home when the noise happened**
- Nari checks ordinary security logic rather than assuming anything supernatural
- available door/smart-lock history shows no ordinary entry during the relevant window
- nothing obvious is stolen and no intruder is found
- a dining chair becomes the first recurring physical continuity marker
- Nari deliberately leaves that chair in a known position before bed
- Chapter 001 contains **no on-page Hyun-woo reveal, no silhouette, no voice, no name, no roommate claim, and no explanation of blackout mechanics**
- Chapter 001 does not present a major reader-visible blackout event
- after Nari is asleep / absent from the living area, the reader alone receives the final proof that the chair has moved or hears it scrape in the otherwise empty apartment
- Nari does not witness the final movement
- creator-only continuity: a brief genuine supplied-power interruption may occur inside the final-night window to preserve the premise-level physical rule, but Chapter 001 must not present that interruption as the answer or causally connect it to the chair for the reader

## Arc 01 — Approved Early Reveal Guardrails

The first stretch of Arc 01 should escalate questions rather than reveal the title premise immediately.

Current pacing guardrail, adjustable by roughly one chapter when scene consequences justify it:

1. Chapter 001 — noise / movement in an apartment that should be empty
2. Chapters 002–004 — ordinary security explanations and false mundane theories fail gradually
3. Chapter 005 — Nari begins noticing a loose relationship between disturbances and electrical irregularities
4. Chapter 006 — a physical change occurs under conditions Nari can no longer dismiss as simple unauthorized entry
5. Chapter 007 — first substantial genuine blackout while Nari is home; unseen presence becomes directly experienced
6. Chapter 008 — first voice / communication without a complete visual reveal
7. Chapters 009–010 — partial then clearer human reveal; identity remains incomplete
8. Chapters 011–13 — observable-condition testing and evidence problems
9. Chapter 014 onward — negotiated coexistence can begin only after enough accumulated conflict and proof

The exact chapter numbers may shift slightly during story development, but the **reveal order must not collapse**.
