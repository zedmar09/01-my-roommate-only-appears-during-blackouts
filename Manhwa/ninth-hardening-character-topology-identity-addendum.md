# Ninth Hardening — Character Topology / Identity-Surface Addendum

This addendum is binding on Chapter 1 Strip 001–032 and extends the current `manhwa-2d-production-auditor` rules.

It does not change story canon. It closes character-rendering loopholes where the correct action, camera, prop and environment can still be shown with broken anatomy, mirrored identity landmarks, dropped wardrobe/accessories, altered tattoo/hair construction, or local-color/material drift between adjacent views.

## Authority

1. current user instruction
2. current chapter/current strip script
3. approved character WebPs
4. approved environment WebPs
5. approved object WebPs
6. current hardened strip prompt
7. previous APPROVED strip temporary state only
8. canon-compatible derived micro-detail only

A generated close-up, reverse shot, blackout rendering, dramatic expression, crop, or partial occlusion may never redefine permanent character identity.

## Whole-Body Anatomical Topology — Absolute

Every visible character must remain one physically coherent human body.

Reject:
- extra/missing arms, forearms, hands, legs, feet, ears, fingers, or duplicated body parts
- detached/floating hand, foot, ear, hair mass, jaw, shoulder, or limb
- fused arms/legs/fingers where the body topology becomes impossible
- elbow/knee/wrist/ankle bending through an impossible joint direction
- shoulder/arm connection that cannot connect to the visible torso
- a hand emerging from the wrong side of the body or an arm that changes owner between wide/detail views
- lower body/feet that cannot connect to the visible pelvis/legs in the same pose
- a close-up limb that is incompatible with the adjacent wide-shot body position

Partial occlusion is allowed only when the hidden connection remains physically possible and consistent with the surrounding body.

## Limb Ownership Across Close-Ups / Reverse Shots — Absolute

When a close-up shows a continuity-critical hand, wrist, foot, arm, or leg:
- it belongs to the SAME character and SAME anatomical side established by the adjacent body view
- preserve sleeve/glove/tattoo/scrunchie/skin/clothing cues needed to identify ownership
- do not create a generic disembodied hand/foot that could belong to another character
- reverse shots may flip screen-left/right but not anatomical ownership
- same-object inserts do not authorize a second hand/arm/foot copy

If ownership is unclear, include an adjacent context slice instead of inventing or mirroring anatomy.

## Permanent Character Landmark Side — Absolute

Character-specific permanent landmarks retain anatomical side under every camera angle.

### Yoon Nari
- exactly ONE tiny black beauty mark beside the LEFT corner of her mouth
- it remains on Nari's anatomical LEFT, even when a reverse shot makes it appear on the opposite screen side
- do not move it to the right, center, cheek, nose, chin, or duplicate it

### Hyun-woo
- tattoo coverage/pattern relationships remain attached to the same anatomical neck/collarbone/shoulder/arm regions
- left/right tattoo relationships may not mirror or migrate because the camera reverses
- technical mask attachment points and low-tied-hair construction remain the same physical construction

### Mrs. Na
- square jade earrings remain the same pair at both ears when visible
- analog wristwatch remains on the same canonical wrist
- dark gloves remain the same gloves unless the script explicitly removes them

### Seungjae
- smartwatch remains the same physical watch/wrist
- one earbud in each ear when worn
- medium-brown hair part/silhouette remains the same across angles

## Face / Hair / Age / Build Identity — Absolute

Close-ups, expressions, lighting changes and perspective may not recast a character.

Preserve:
- facial proportions and canonical eye/nose/jaw relationships
- age presentation
- hair length, part, tie, fringe and silhouette
- height/build relationship
- permanent marks/accessories

Reject:
- Nari becoming teenage, doll-like, masculine-recast, short-haired, black-haired/brown-haired instead of dark-plum, or losing the left beauty mark
- Hyun-woo becoming slim/short-haired/small-bun/unmasked/different-mask/different tattoo coverage
- Mrs. Na becoming young/glamorous/frail/witch-like or losing her mature face/silver twist identity
- Seungjae appearing older than 30, middle-aged, executive/paternal, bearded/stubbled, gray/receding-haired, or visually merging with Hyun-woo

Expression changes must happen on the SAME face rather than creating a different person.

## Wardrobe Construction / Local-Color Continuity — Absolute

Within one continuous scene, clothing and signature local colors remain the same physical items/colors unless a legal time/location cut or scripted wardrobe change establishes otherwise.

Preserve at minimum when applicable:
- Nari: dark-plum hair, dark graphic hoodie, loose black trousers, yellow scrunchie, headphones; outside/work jacket/bag only when the scene establishes them
- Hyun-woo: matte-black mask/top, dark utility trousers, black low-tied hair, tattoo coverage
- Mrs. Na: muted-mauve coat, dark gloves, jade earrings, analog watch, handbag
- Seungjae: medium-brown hair, one consistent youthful office-casual outfit, smartwatch/earbuds/umbrella/phone identities

Lighting may shift value/saturation naturally, but it may not change identity hue or material:
- dark-plum hair must not become pure black, brown, red or neon purple
- yellow scrunchie must not become another color
- Mrs. Na's mauve coat/jade earrings may not change palette between reverse shots
- Hyun-woo tattoos/mask may not glow neon or change pattern/color
- Seungjae's hair/outfit may not change color/construction between adjacent shots

## Clothing / Body Occlusion Topology — Absolute

Clothing must wrap one continuous body.

Reject:
- sleeves attaching to the wrong arm
- hoodie/jacket collar cutting through neck/jaw/headphones/hair
- trousers/coat/hoodie creating duplicate or missing limbs
- fabric folds implying an extra hidden arm/leg
- coat/bag/hoodie occlusion used to hide an impossible body connection
- tattoos visible through opaque clothing except at genuinely exposed skin

Flat 2D simplification is allowed; impossible garment/body topology is not.

## Speaking / Listening / Mouth-State Logic — Absolute

Dialogue ownership must agree with visible acting.

For human speech:
- the speaking character's face/head/body orientation must support that line
- do not make the listener visibly mouth the speaker's line
- do not make both characters appear to speak the same line simultaneously unless script explicitly overlaps dialogue
- a closed/neutral listener mouth is preferred when dialogue ownership would otherwise become ambiguous

For internal thought/narration/device/speaker audio:
- do not animate a human mouth as if the line is spoken aloud
- Hyun-woo voice-only through the smart speaker must not create a physical speaking mouth/body while Unit power is on
- masked Hyun-woo speech must respect the mask; do not expose or redraw the lower face merely to show speaking

Mouth animation is not required in every panel, but visible acting must never contradict source ownership.

## Character Local-Material Continuity — Absolute

Skin, hair, cloth, mask, tattoo, jewelry and accessories retain the same matte flat-2D material family across adjacent views.

Reject:
- one close-up suddenly giving glossy skin/lips/hair while wides are matte
- jade earrings or watch becoming mirror-chrome/glowing
- mask becoming shiny plastic/metal between shots
- tattoo turning into luminous circuitry
- hair becoming wet/glossy without a physical cause
- local color/material changes used only to make a close-up more dramatic

## Ninth-Pass Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- extra/missing/duplicated limb/body part appears
- limb ownership or anatomical side changes between wide/detail views
- a close-up hand/foot cannot physically connect to the same character body
- Nari's beauty mark is missing, duplicated, or appears on anatomical right
- character face/age/build/hair identity drifts under expression/reverse shot
- Mrs. Na loses/switches gloves, earrings or watch without action
- Seungjae visually ages or changes hair/outfit identity
- Hyun-woo tattoo map, mask construction or low-tied hair changes/mirrors
- wardrobe construction or signature local colors change without legal cut/scripted change
- clothing occlusion hides impossible anatomy
- a listener visibly mouths another character's line or internal/device/audio text is acted as spoken human dialogue
- local character materials become glossy/neon/cinematic between adjacent views

These are prompt/preproduction gates. Actual rendered compliance remains PENDING until image pixels are inspected.
