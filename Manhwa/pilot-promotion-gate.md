# Manhwa Pilot Promotion Gate

The manhwa test must not replace the existing `Comics/` package automatically.

## Required Before Promotion

1. All 15 Chapter 1 technical strips generated and approved.
2. Full 15-strip stitch completed with no visible seams.
3. Exact source script preserved.
4. Canonical character identities stable throughout.
5. Unit 2407 geography and props remain coherent.
6. Helpful/hostile device signatures remain distinct.
7. Mobile lettering remains readable at final publishing width.
8. Vertical pacing feels materially better than the page-based version.
9. The Chapter 1 ending at the 12:43 A.M. blackout works as a satisfying serialized hook.
10. User explicitly approves the manhwa format for promotion.

## Only After Explicit Approval

Then we may design a migration plan to replace or archive the older manga/comic presentation. Until then, `Manhwa/` is an isolated experiment and `Comics/` remains unchanged.
