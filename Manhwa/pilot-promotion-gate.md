# Manhwa Pilot Promotion Gate

The manhwa test must not replace the existing `Comics/` package automatically.

## Required Before Promotion

1. All 32 Chapter 1 technical strips generated and approved.
2. Full 32-strip stitch completed with no visible seams.
3. Source Comic Chapter 1 Pages 001–018 fully represented with exact approved reader-facing script.
4. No huge purposeless bottom whitespace or double-gap seams remain.
5. Canonical character identities remain stable throughout.
6. Chapter 1 environment canonical packs are generated/approved and Unit 2407/building shared-area geography matches them throughout.
7. Chapter 1 speaker/TV/refrigerator object canonicals are generated/approved and recurring object designs/states remain stable.
8. Unit 2407 geography, movement, props, and power states remain coherent.
9. Hyun-woo appears physically only during the actual Unit 2407 power-loss sequence and is voice-only/absent when powered.
10. Helpful/hostile device signatures remain distinct; TV and refrigerator use their separately documented hostile manifestation languages.
11. Mobile lettering remains readable at final publishing width.
12. Vertical pacing feels materially better than the page-based version.
13. Final `NEW TENANT CONFIRMED` cliffhanger works as the Chapter 1 ending.
14. User explicitly approves the manhwa format for promotion.

## Only After Explicit Approval

Then we may design a migration plan to replace or archive the older manga/comic presentation. Until then, `Manhwa/` remains isolated and `Comics/` remains unchanged.
