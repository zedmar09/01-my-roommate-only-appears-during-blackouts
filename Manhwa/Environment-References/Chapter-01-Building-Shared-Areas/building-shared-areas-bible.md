# Building Shared-Areas Bible

## Locked Route

1. exterior under residential entrance canopy
2. inward crossing through the SAME glass entry doors
3. ground-floor lobby movement deeper inside
4. elevator bank
5. return path from elevator bank toward same glass entrance

## Canonical Anchors

- modern residential lobby, not hotel/office mall
- glass entrance / rainy exterior relation
- elevator bank position and number of visible elevator doors chosen once and frozen
- simple matte stone/color-block walls
- elevator call controls
- modest seating/side table/plant elements if used, frozen after approval
- no ads, store signs, random branding, or glossy mirror walls

## Direction Rule

Strips 012–013 must never flip the lobby. Entrance is behind after entry; elevator is ahead/deeper inside. A reverse shot may change screen direction but must remain geometrically truthful to the canonical map.
