# Seam Continuity Protocol

## Purpose

Technical image boundaries must disappear after stitching. The reader should never be able to identify where `strip-###` ends.

## Canonical Anchor Rule

Before evaluating a seam, compare both strips against the relevant approved environment and core-object canonicals.

A seam is invalid even if Strip N visually matches Strip N+1 when both have drifted away from the canonical room/object design. Do not preserve a mistake merely because it is consistent across two generated strips.

For apartment seams verify fixed landmarks such as walls, doors, windows, table, fridge, TV zone, cabinet, desk, hallway axis, and relevant object bodies against the canonical reference pack.

## Seam Types

### G — Gutter Seam
Use only when a natural quiet transition exists. The gutter must be compact or meaningfully paced; it is not permission for an unused blank tail.

### A — Artwork-Continuation Seam
The lower portion of Strip N and upper portion of Strip N+1 depict the same continuing environment/composition/action.

### E — Effect/Atmosphere Seam
Use for rain, darkness, glitch fragments, city lights, candle smoke, hostile pixels, or other continuing fields.

## Previous-Strip Attachment Rule

For every strip after 001, attach the immediately previous APPROVED manhwa strip plus relevant canonical environment/object references.

For A/E seams preserve camera axis/perspective, object scale, canonical wall/floor/background alignment, character identity/wardrobe, continuing pose/movement, power/lighting state, reader-background tone, and recurring object design/state.

## No-Dead-Space Seam Rule — Absolute

Never create a seam by simply leaving a huge empty lower area.

If a story beat needs a pause, that pause must read intentionally through existing narration, existing scripted SFX, reaction, canonical environmental continuation, atmosphere, or reveal timing.

If there is no such purpose, continue the artwork closer to the technical edge and use only a small buffer. Never solve blank space by inventing new narration, dialogue, or SFX.

Reject a double-gap join where Strip N has excessive empty bottom space and Strip N+1 also begins with excessive empty top space.

## Overlap Strategy

When practical, repeat a small lower-edge continuity zone from Strip N at the top of Strip N+1. Crop the duplicate during final stitching. Recommended overlap concept: roughly 10–20% of the transition area, never a reader-visible repeated panel. Do not repeat dialogue or SFX inside the overlap.

## Text Seam Rule — Absolute

Never split across a seam: speech balloon, narration box, handwritten message, device text, critical SFX word, or a face if mismatch would be obvious.

A background, body below the face, furniture, rain, dark room, city field, or tall effect may continue when reliable.

## Seam QA

Before approval compare bottom of Strip N to top of Strip N+1:
- width identical
- canonical environment landmarks remain correct
- recurring object body/state remains canonical
- no accidental horizontal white/black line
- no duplicated text
- no missing floor/wall segment
- no changed appliance geometry
- no power/lighting jump unless scripted
- no changed character scale
- no pose teleport
- no excessive dead bottom/top gap

If the join is obvious at normal phone-scroll speed or conflicts with a canonical reference, reject/regenerate the newer strip.
