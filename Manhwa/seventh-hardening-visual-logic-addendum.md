# Seventh Hardening — Visual Logic Addendum

This addendum is binding on Chapter 1 Strip 001–032 and any later production prompt that adopts the same `manhwa-2d-production-auditor` rules.

It does not replace character/environment/object canon. It adds stricter visual-logic gates for camera, gaze, physical text, reflections/shadows, grip ergonomics, and worn-object attachment.

## Authority

1. current user instruction
2. current chapter/current strip script
3. approved character WebPs
4. approved environment WebPs
5. approved object WebPs
6. current hardened strip prompt
7. previous APPROVED strip temporary state only
8. canon-compatible derived micro-detail only

No previous render, camera convenience, reflection, shadow, close-up, or stylization may redefine permanent canon.

## Camera Axis / Eyeline Continuity — Absolute

Camera direction may change; story-world positions and gaze targets may not.

For same continuous conversation/action:
- preserve each character's world-space side, facing, and distance unless a visible movement changes it
- a reverse shot may flip screen-left/screen-right, but it may not place a character on the opposite physical side of the room/table/door
- eyelines must terminate toward the real story-world target: speaker, refrigerator, TV, hallway lamp, other character, cabinet, phone, notebook, etc.
- do not make a character visually look away from the object/person they are reacting to merely because the camera reversed
- if a camera intentionally crosses the 180-degree action/conversation axis, first use a neutral/establishing/reorientation view that makes the new axis readable; never silently flip the axis inside one dialogue/action exchange
- balloon tails do not substitute for correct head/eye direction

Reject a locally attractive reverse shot that makes gaze geography impossible.

## Physical Text-Plane Lock — Absolute

In-world text belongs to the physical plane that carries it.

Applies to:
- TV/refrigerator/tablet/phone/smart-lock screens
- binder/manual pages
- handwritten paper/note/notebook
- labels that genuinely exist on an object

Rules:
- text must remain inside the real screen/page/object boundary
- text perspective/orientation follows the carrier plane; it does not stay perfectly front-facing when the physical screen/page is oblique
- page/screen text may be enlarged in a representational close-up, but the close-up is the SAME physical object and must not imply a floating UI in world space
- text cannot detach from its screen/page, float over the room, cross a bezel/page edge, or remain visible after the carrier is turned away/closed/dark
- handwriting must sit on paper fibers/page plane and follow the physical writing path; it is not a digital overlay
- device text cannot become narration or speech merely because a close-up is used

## Reflection / Shadow False-Duplicate Ban — Absolute

The flat matte manhwa style does not authorize invented reflective doubles or horror silhouettes.

Unless the script explicitly calls for a reflection/shadow as story information:
- do not create an identifiable reflected duplicate of Nari, Hyun-woo, Mrs. Na, Seungjae, or any plot object in TV glass, refrigerator doors, windows, glass doors, polished counters, phone/tablet screens, or other surfaces
- matte TV/fridge/screens remain non-mirror-like
- windows/glass may have restrained environmental sheen, but no readable second human figure
- do not invent a shadow-person, silhouette, face, hand, or figure that is not physically present
- cast shadows must originate only from actual current light sources and remain consistent with object/body position
- during blackout, no shadow may imply an unpowered/invented light source
- after the candle is extinguished, candle-cast shadows disappear; fridge pixels do not create giant human shadows
- a power flicker may alter light/shadow briefly but may not duplicate or teleport a person/object

Any false reflected/shadow duplicate of a character or evidence object is automatic rejection.

## Grip / Finger / Contact Ergonomics — Absolute

Critical handling must be mechanically usable, not merely overlapping shapes.

For plug, key, key ring, knife, sheath, pen, chopsticks, phone, umbrella, bag handle, mask hardware, cabinet/door handle, chair, packet, lid, flashlight, radio:
- use believable finger count and joint direction
- fingers wrap/contact the graspable surface; they do not penetrate the object or fuse together
- a plug is gripped by plug body, not prongs or cable
- a knife is gripped by handle, never blade
- a pen/chopstick grip is plausible for writing/eating/holding
- a phone is supported by palm/fingers or surface, not floating against fingertips
- a bag/umbrella handle is actually enclosed/supported by hand/arm
- a key-ring detachment must give fingers enough access to the ring/key; do not melt key/ring/fingers together
- door/cabinet hardware contact must occur at a usable handle/edge, not a hand pasted onto the panel

Minor hand simplification is allowed for flat 2D style; impossible anatomy/contact is not.

## Clothing / Accessory / Strap Attachment — Absolute

Worn items must physically attach to the body/clothing they occupy.

Applies to:
- Nari's yellow scrunchie
- headphones around Nari's neck
- shoulder bags and straps
- Seungjae's watch/earbuds
- Mrs. Na's handbag
- Hyun-woo's mask straps/hardware
- hoodie/coat/hair interactions around these items

Rules:
- scrunchie encircles the same wrist; it does not float beside, sink into, or jump through the arm
- headphones rest around neck/collar/hoodie with plausible depth; earcups/band do not pass through neck, jaw, hair, hoodie, or each other
- bag straps follow the correct shoulder/body contour and may be partly occluded by hair/clothing without changing sides
- watch wraps the same wrist; earbuds sit in the same ears when worn
- mask straps/hardware remain attached to actual mask/head route and do not pass through ears/hair/head
- hair/clothing may overlap visually only in physically plausible front/behind order

Occlusion is allowed. Clipping, floating, side swap, disappearance, or duplicate worn item is rejection.

## Audio Source / Reaction Direction — Absolute

For offscreen or device audio:
- establish the real source location in story space
- SFX/voice lettering placement points to that source without becoming a floating physical object
- character head/eyes/body reaction turns toward the real source
- a new camera angle cannot move the sound source to a different wall/cabinet/device

Especially enforce speaker voice, cabinet chimes, `SLURP`, refrigerator/TV sounds, smart lock, and appliance SFX.

## Weather / Glass Boundary — Absolute

When exterior weather and interior glass architecture coexist:
- rain remains on the exterior side of the building envelope
- after characters cross indoors, active rainfall is behind/outside the glass; only plausible residual wetness on umbrella/clothing/floor may remain
- glass reflections must obey Reflection / Shadow False-Duplicate Ban
- a glass door/window cannot become an invisible portal that erases the threshold/frame

## Occlusion Cannot Replace Proof — Absolute

A continuity-critical fact may be temporarily occluded only after it has already been physically established.

Do not use a foreground body, balloon, darkness, hair, furniture, or close crop to avoid drawing:
- plug/socket disconnection
- key transfer
- tool return
- mask movement
- chair sit/stand
- step/packet contact
- knife fall/impact
- wrist grip/release
- phone/notebook placement
- door/elevator threshold crossing

If the decisive action would be hidden, recompose.

## Seventh-Pass Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- same conversation/action silently crosses 180° axis and makes eyelines/world sides inconsistent
- character looks toward wrong story-world target
- device/page text floats off its carrier plane or ignores perspective/boundary
- reflection/shadow invents a duplicate person, figure, hand, face, or evidence object
- shadow implies a nonexistent light source
- critical grip is anatomically unusable or fingers/object clip/fuse
- scrunchie/headphones/bag strap/watch/earbud/mask strap floats, clips, swaps side, or detaches without action
- offscreen/device sound appears to come from wrong place
- rain/weather crosses the building envelope impossibly
- occlusion/crop is used to evade required physical action proof

These are prompt/preproduction gates. Actual rendered compliance remains PENDING until pixels are inspected.
