## Opening Conversion Rule

Strip 001 is the reference example for how source manga/comic pages are converted into vertical manhwa: preserve the FULL approved source-page story, but recompose its old page panels into one fluid top-to-bottom read.

Comic Page 1 is therefore entirely contained in Strip 001. The technical seam occurs only after Nari's `GREAT. IT TALKS.` reaction.

## Whitespace Rule

No strip receives a giant empty bottom tail simply to reserve a seam. A G seam is a controlled reader-space transition, not unused canvas. If a strip ends early, extend meaningful composition/environment/atmosphere or use only a small seam buffer.
