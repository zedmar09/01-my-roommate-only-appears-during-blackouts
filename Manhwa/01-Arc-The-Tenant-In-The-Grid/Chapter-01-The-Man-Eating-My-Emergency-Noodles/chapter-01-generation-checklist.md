## Black Read-Slice Gate — Absolute

Inspect every distinct slice in every strip:
- SMALL BLACK GUTTER between consecutive distinct vertical slices
- diagonal/slanted BLACK DIVIDER between two distinct camera slices sharing one horizontal row
