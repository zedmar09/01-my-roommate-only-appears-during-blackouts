## Ninth-Pass Character Topology / Identity Gates

### Whole-Body Topology
Every character remains one coherent human body. No extra/missing/duplicated arms, hands, legs, feet, ears, fingers, detached body parts or impossible joints.

### Limb Ownership
Close-up hands/wrists/feet/arms/legs remain attached to the SAME character and SAME anatomical side. Reverse shots may flip screen direction but cannot switch limb ownership.

### Permanent Character Landmarks
- Nari: exactly one beauty mark beside anatomical LEFT mouth corner; same long dark-plum hair
- Hyun-woo: same tattoo map by anatomical side, same matte technical mask, same long low-tied black hair
- Mrs. Na: same jade earrings, watch wrist and dark gloves
- Seungjae: exactly age 30, same medium-brown hair silhouette, watch wrist and earbud identities

### Face / Age / Build / Hair
Close-up, expression, darkness and lighting cannot recast the character. Nari never reverts to short hair; Seungjae never becomes older/paternal/executive; Hyun-woo never becomes slim/short-haired/differently masked/tattooed; Mrs. Na never becomes young/glamorous.

### Wardrobe / Local Color / Material
Within a continuous scene, clothing construction and signature colors stay stable. Lighting changes value only. Character surfaces remain flat/matte; no glossy close-up, neon tattoo, shiny mask/jewelry or arbitrary hair recolor.

### Clothing / Body Occlusion
Sleeves, trousers, coats, hoodie, hair, headphones, bag and mask straps attach to one continuous body. Occlusion cannot hide an extra/missing limb or impossible body connection.

### Speaking / Listening / Mouth State
Visible acting follows source ownership. A listener cannot mouth another character's line. Narration/internal thought/device text/speaker audio is not acted as human speech. Masked Hyun-woo does not expose his lower face merely to show speech.
