## Eighth-Pass Presentation Gates

### Perspective / Lens Integrity
No fisheye, ultra-wide or forced-perspective distortion that bends canonical geometry or changes real body/object proportions. Dramatic presentation uses framing/shot size, not world-space warping.

### Mobile-Scale Evidence
Continuity-critical proof must be understandable at normal mobile reading size. A tiny technically-present plug, key, page edge, foot, chair leg, knife, wrist, cabinet layer, handwritten line or TV message does not pass.

### Crop / Gutter / Divider Safety
Critical contacts and evidence receive safe margins. Canvas edges, black gutters, diagonal dividers and technical seams may not cut or obscure decisive mechanisms.

### Lighting / Shadow Direction
Same lighting state = same physically coherent source direction. Candle light ends when the candle goes out; fridge/TV/speaker pixels do not become room lights; restored powered lighting keeps one baseline after return.

### Top-to-Bottom Reader Order
Action, dialogue, SFX, narration and device text must read in exact scripted causal order. A result/SFX/response cannot visually precede its cause.

### Critical Limb / Object Ownership
Close-ups preserve enough context to prove which arm/foot/object/cord/notebook the detail belongs to and retain anatomical/story-world orientation.

### Readable In-World Text
Exact story-critical device/page/note/notebook wording remains legible at intended mobile size and attached to its physical screen/page plane.
