## Tenth / Final Performance Gates

A camera cut is not a pose reset. Standing/seated/crouched/walking/leaning/shielding state, weight-bearing feet, shoulders, hips, torso facing and current gesture persist until visible action/legal cut changes them.

### Relative Height / Build
World-space character proportions remain stable. Hyun-woo stays clearly taller/broader than Nari when both stand on the same floor plane. Seungjae remains a lean-to-average same-generation peer and less broad/intimidating than Hyun-woo. Camera framing may not resize people for drama.

### Emotion / Performance
Emotional intensity progresses from actual events instead of resetting at reverse shots. Use grounded adult acting:
- dry/practical move-in
- skeptical analytical early evidence
- ordinary Seungjae warmth/confusion
- controlled blackout alertness
- guarded caution/interrogation
- focused protective urgency
- localized shock at knife/wrist event
- shaken but functional aftermath
- controlled dread at final TV message

No unscripted screaming, crying, chibi shock, villain smirk, romantic glamour or exaggerated pose.

### Gesture / Clothing Layer / Hair Motion
Temporary gestures persist until changed. Hood/jacket/sleeve/bag/headphones/mask wear state cannot silently change at camera cuts. Hair/clothing may sway naturally but cannot redesign itself or hide an unperformed body turn.

### Seam Performance
Every strip boundary carries pose, facing, gesture, relative scale, emotional intensity and clothing-layer state until a legal cut or visible first action changes them.
