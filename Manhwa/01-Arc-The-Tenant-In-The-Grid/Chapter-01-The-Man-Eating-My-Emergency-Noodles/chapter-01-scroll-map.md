Source: Chapter 1 comic Pages 001–018.

## Sequence 001 — Move-In / Smart-Home Hook

Story beats:
- Unit 2407 is suspiciously cheap.
- Nari arrives with boxes/tablet.
- smart lock opens.
- apartment is surprisingly good for the price.
- smart-home montage.
- screenless speaker welcomes her by name.
- Nari: `GREAT. IT TALKS.`

Mood: ordinary with a faint wrongness, not horror yet.

## Sequence 002 — Mrs. Na's Warning

Story beats:
- later that evening
- doorbell / Mrs. Na arrives
- binder + brass backup key
- hallway-light rule
- apartment-light requirement; phone/tablet do not count
- whole-building-outage question
- Mrs. Na hesitates
- `THEN DON'T TURN ON A SCREEN.`
- wait for power / call her
- Mrs. Na refuses full explanation and leaves

Mood: grounded conversation becoming quietly serious.

## Sequence 003 — The Speaker Answers Back

Story beats:
- one-week automation annoyance montage
- nighttime kitchen
- normal synthetic assistant fails to understand
- light flicker / chopsticks fall
- unexpected low natural male voice: `YOU COULD SAY PLEASE.`
- Nari freezes and asks who spoke
- no answer
- Nari unplugs speaker

Mood: first undeniable human intrusion.

## Sequence 004 — TV / Refrigerator

Use clean helpful vs hostile signatures as separate visual languages. Do not merge them into one generic glitch effect.

## Sequence 005 — Paper Only

This is the first sequence where physical writing becomes a recurring visual motif. Give paper texture and handwriting enough close-up space to read naturally.

## Sequence 006 — Ordinary Life Before Outage

The store and rainy walk-home sequence should briefly open up the chapter visually. Seungjae must feel safe/normal; the later device failures are the intrusion.

## Sequence 007 — Blackout / Noodle Reveal

This is the main Chapter 1 scroll-horror showcase. Use black gutters and delay the source of `SLURP`.

The full physical Hyun-woo silhouette should not be revealed until after Nari has followed the sound into the dining area.

## Sequence 008 — Hyun-Woo

Keep Hyun-woo seated for the initial exchange. His physical calm contrasts with Nari's knife-held caution.

## Sequence 009 — Hostile System

This is the most visually aggressive sequence. Maintain readable geography and never let glitch effects obscure Nari/Hyun-woo positions.

## Sequence 010 — Gone

Hyun-woo's disappearance is not a transformation. After power returns, show the same space simply empty.

## Sequence 011 — Borrowed Current

Return to quieter pacing. The disconnected cord is crucial proof. Hyun-woo remains physically absent.

## Sequence 012 — Cliffhanger

End with the notebook hypothesis still fresh in the reader's mind, then contradict Nari's sense of control with the hostile unplugged TV.

Do not add a chapter-end title card after the hostile reveal unless separately approved.
