## Continuous Rhythm

### Top third — ordinary reality becoming wrong
Runs A–F use mostly light-neutral gutter/background. Keep dialogue fairly compact and environments readable. Suspense grows by isolating objects rather than making the whole chapter visually dark too early.

### Middle — outage and physical manifestation
Runs G–I are the chapter's main horror descent. Gradually move the reader column into charcoal/black. Let darkness itself occupy vertical space. Do not reveal Hyun-woo before the slurping/dining geography has been established.

### Bottom third — rules, disappearance, evidence, cliffhanger
Runs J–L reduce visual aggression after the hostile refrigerator sequence, then rebuild tension through investigation and paper evidence. The last hostile TV reveal gets the longest clean reveal gap of the chapter.

## Major Reveal Spacing

Give materially larger vertical breathing room before:
- first natural male speaker voice
- TV remaining active while visibly unplugged
- physical handwritten reply
- actual blackout
- first unexplained `SLURP`
- physical Hyun-woo silhouette/reveal
- hostile `YOON NARI`
- `IDENTITY LOCKING`
- empty space after Hyun-woo disappears
- disconnected speaker talking again
- final `NEW TENANT CONFIRMED`

## No Visible Technical Boundaries

Technical production strips may start/end inside safe gutters, but the final stitch must preserve these Story Runs as one seamless flow. Never add labels such as `Run G`, `Strip 7`, `Page 10`, or `Part 2` to reader-facing art.
