# Manhwa Production Readiness Gate

The Manhwa pipeline is now the permanent production format.

A chapter is production-ready only when:
1. its full vertical beat plan and strip manifest are locked
2. exact reader-facing text/source ownership is locked
3. required character canonicals are approved
4. all recurring environments have approved canonical packs
5. plot-critical recurring objects have approved canonical/state packs
6. every technical strip is generated and approved sequentially
7. movement/object routes and environment geometry remain coherent
8. no huge purposeless bottom whitespace or double-gap seams remain
9. helpful/hostile signatures and object-specific effects remain correct
10. mobile lettering remains readable
11. the stitched chapter has no visible seams or unplanned power/lighting jumps
12. final chapter ending is approved

Do not reintroduce the retired traditional manga/page-grid production pipeline.
