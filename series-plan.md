# My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts - Series Plan

This file stores the long-running manga/manhwa roadmap for `My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts`.

Creator-only spoiler note: this file may contain long-range mystery mechanics, hidden culprit structure, Hyun-woo's full origin, memory-transfer costs, body-restoration rules, and climax details. Do not copy those reveals into app metadata, promo copy, early page prompts, public chapter listings, or reader-facing summaries until the story has earned them on-page.

The current production structure should stay focused on the chapters being written or generated. Do not create future arc folders until we are ready to start that arc.

## Long-Running Target

Recommended long-running version:

- Total arcs: 8
- Total chapters: 120
- Average arc length: 15 chapters
- Format: supernatural romantic comedy manhwa with smart-home comedy, blackout-only domestic romance, urban fantasy cases, memory horror, and a final body-restoration conflict

This is the preferred long-running target before considering a 150+ chapter version.

## Story Engine

The story can support 100+ chapters because it has three repeating engines:

- Romance engine: Nari and Hyun-woo only get real physical time together during blackouts.
- Mystery engine: Nari investigates who caused Hyun-woo's grid accident, why his records disappeared, and what the Black Surge really is.
- Urban fantasy engine: smart devices, cameras, building systems, phones, elevators, and city infrastructure become supernatural case material.

Each arc should introduce a new kind of blackout problem. Avoid repeating the same "haunted appliance" gag without a new clue, emotional cost, or relationship shift.

## Mystery Spine

The direct supernatural culprit is `The Black Surge`: an intelligent electrical entity that consumes memory traces from devices and people.

The long-running version should also keep a human culprit behind the accident:

- A director, engineer, company founder, or government contractor behind the experimental grid project chose to keep the test running, hide the accident, or sacrifice Hyun-woo to protect the project.
- The landlord, Na Young-sook, should remain a guilty witness rather than the final villain.
- Maintenance chief Yang Chulho should help Nari uncover the old wiring, missing maintenance logs, and modified breaker routes.

How Hyun-woo ended in the grid:

Hyun-woo was an electrical-grid engineer investigating unexplained failures across Seoul. He discovered that the experimental power network was not only moving electricity; it was also collecting memory traces from smart devices, cameras, phones, voice assistants, and building systems. During a midnight test at the underground grid site, he tried to shut the system down manually. The human culprit kept the project running or locked him inside to protect the experiment. The Black Surge used Hyun-woo's body as a grounding point, pulled his physical existence into the electrical network, and erased his records and memories from the world.

## Expansion Option

If the series needs more room after the 120-chapter plan, expand toward 150 chapters by adding case arcs between major mystery arcs.

Good expansion slots:

- Between Arc 2 and Arc 3: short cases about people partially forgotten by the city.
- Between Arc 3 and Arc 4: device-possession incidents tied to different buildings.
- Between Arc 5 and Arc 6: old tenant stories from Unit 2407.
- Between Arc 6 and Arc 7: rival infrastructure teams, media attention, and false explanations for the blackout district.

Avoid jumping directly to 200+ chapters unless the story gains several strong recurring factions, a broader city conspiracy, or parallel couples affected by the same memory-grid system.

## Future Folder Rule

```text
Comics/
  02-Arc-The-City-That-Forgot-Him/
    README.md
    Chapter-16-Title-In-Camel-Case/
      chapter.md
      page-001-chatgpt-image-prompt.md
      1.webp
```

Each chapter folder should contain:

- `chapter.md`
- page prompt Markdown files
- generated PNG files and manually converted final reader WebP files

Shared `story_cover`, `front`, and `back` prompts/assets belong in `Covers/`, not inside individual chapter folders.
