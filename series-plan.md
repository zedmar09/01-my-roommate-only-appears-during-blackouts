# My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts — Series Plan

This file stores the long-running Manhwa roadmap for `My Roommate Only Appears During Blackouts`.

Creator-only spoiler note: this file may contain long-range mystery mechanics, hidden culprit structure, Hyun-woo's full origin, memory-transfer costs, body-restoration rules, and climax details. Do not copy those reveals into reader-facing chapter copy before the story earns them.

## Long-Running Target

- Total arcs: 8
- Total chapters: 120
- Average arc length: 15 chapters
- Format: continuous vertical supernatural romantic-comedy Manhwa with smart-home comedy, blackout-only domestic romance, urban fantasy cases, memory horror, and a final body-restoration conflict

## Story Engine

- Romance engine: Nari and Hyun-woo get physical time together only during real Unit 2407 power-loss conditions.
- Mystery engine: Nari investigates who caused Hyun-woo's grid accident, why his records disappeared, and what the Black Surge really is.
- Urban-fantasy engine: smart devices, cameras, elevators, phones, building systems, and city infrastructure become supernatural case material.

Each arc should introduce a new blackout problem, clue, emotional cost, or relationship shift rather than repeating the same haunted-appliance joke.

## Mystery Spine

The direct supernatural culprit is `The Black Surge`, an intelligent electrical entity that consumes memory traces from devices and people.

The long-running version also keeps a human culprit behind the accident: a director, engineer, founder, or contractor behind the experimental grid project chose to keep the test running, hide the accident, or sacrifice Hyun-woo to protect the project.

Na Young-sook remains a guilty witness rather than the final villain. Maintenance chief Yang Chulho helps Nari uncover old wiring, missing maintenance logs, and modified breaker routes.

Hyun-woo was an electrical-grid engineer investigating unexplained failures across Seoul. He discovered an experimental power network collecting memory traces from smart devices, cameras, phones, voice assistants, and building systems. During a midnight test he tried to shut it down manually. The human culprit kept the system running or locked him inside. The Black Surge used his body as a grounding point, pulled his physical existence into the network, and erased his records and memories from the world.

## Future Manhwa Folder Rule

When a new chapter or arc starts, create it under `Manhwa/`.

Example:
```text
Manhwa/
  02-Arc-The-City-That-Forgot-Him/
    Chapter-16-Title-In-Camel-Case/
      README.md
      chapter-16-continuous-scroll-plan.md
      chapter-16-strip-manifest.md
      chapter-16-generation-checklist.md
      Production-Strips/
```

Before generating a new chapter:
- inventory new/reused characters
- inventory every environment and approve canonical packs for new recurring locations
- identify plot-critical recurring objects and approve object canonicals
- design the continuous vertical beat plan and technical-strip manifest
- generate strictly sequentially using the immediately previous approved strip

Do not create traditional page-grid manga production folders again.
